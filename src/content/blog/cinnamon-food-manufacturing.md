**Food manufacturers choose Ceylon cinnamon for its delicate flavour, clean-label origin story and naturally low coumarin, which simplifies compliance with coumarin limits.** Using it well in production comes down to the right format and a tight specification.

## Common applications

| Category | Typical format | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Breakfast cereals and granola | Powder | EU coumarin limit for breakfast cereals is 20 mg/kg |
| Bakery | Powder | Limits apply to traditional/seasonal and fine bakery ware |
| Dairy and desserts | Powder, infusion | EU desserts limit is 5 mg/kg |
| Snacks and confectionery | Powder | See [confectionery](/blog/cinnamon-in-confectionery-chocolate) |
| Beverages | Powder, extracts, infusion | See [beverages](/blog/cinnamon-in-beverages-coffee) |
| Seasonings and blends | Powder | See [spice blends](/blog/cinnamon-spice-blends) |

Coumarin limits are from Regulation (EC) No 1334/2008. See [coumarin explained](/blog/coumarin-in-cinnamon-explained).

## Why Ceylon helps with coumarin

Coumarin in a finished food depends on the cinnamon's coumarin level and the dosage. Cassia's naturally high and variable coumarin can force manufacturers to cap cinnamon levels. Ceylon's naturally low coumarin gives freedom to use enough cinnamon for flavour.

## Process considerations

- **Kill step:** if cinnamon is added after the last heat step (for example, toppings or coatings), use **steam-sterilised** powder. See [steam sterilisation](/blog/steam-sterilisation-vs-irradiation-spices).
- **Heat losses:** aroma decreases in baking and extrusion; test dosage in real production.
- **Dispersion:** pre-blend with sugar or flour for even distribution.
- **Dust control:** cinnamon powder is fine; manage dust in mixing areas.

## Cost management

- Use **powder milled from quillings and featherings** rather than fine quills. See [by-grades](/blog/quillings-featherings-chips).
- **Optimise dosage** by measuring volatile oil.
- **Contract supply** for price stability. See [annual contracts](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).

## Labelling

"Cinnamon" is the minimum; "Ceylon cinnamon" is a selling point if supply is consistent and documented. Keep claims factual. See [labelling](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).

## Summary

Ceylon cinnamon gives manufacturers flavour, a clean origin story and coumarin headroom. Specify it tightly, choose treated powder where needed, and validate in real production.
