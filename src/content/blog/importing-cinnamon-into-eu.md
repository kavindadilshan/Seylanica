**To import Ceylon cinnamon into the EU, you need to meet EU food law as the responsible food business operator, clear customs with the right origin and classification, and be able to prove each lot is safe and correctly labelled.** This checklist covers the main areas. Rules change, so confirm current requirements with your national authority or a customs broker.

## 1. Your role and registrations

- **EORI number** for customs.
- **Food business operator registration** with your national competent authority, where required.
- **Food safety management** based on HACCP principles.
- **Traceability:** one step back, one step forward, under Regulation (EC) No 178/2002.

## 2. Product safety rules

| Area | Main reference | What to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pesticide residues | Reg. (EC) No 396/2005 | Results below EU MRLs. See [pesticide residues](/blog/pesticide-residues-eu-mrl-cinnamon) |
| Contaminants | Reg. (EU) 2023/915 | Heavy metals, mycotoxins where applicable. See [heavy metals](/blog/heavy-metals-spices-testing) |
| Microbiology | Food safety obligations, buyer specs | Salmonella absent in 25 g. See [microbiology](/blog/microbiology-testing-spices) |
| Flavourings | Reg. (EC) No 1334/2008 | Coumarin limits in finished foods. See [coumarin](/blog/coumarin-in-cinnamon-explained) |
| Treatments | EU irradiation rules; EtO not permitted | See [steam vs irradiation vs EtO](/blog/steam-sterilisation-vs-irradiation-spices) |

## 3. Plant health

Check whether a phytosanitary certificate is required for your product form under current EU plant health rules. Many importers request one regardless. See [the phytosanitary certificate](/blog/phytosanitary-certificate-npqs).

## 4. Customs and origin

- **Classification:** HS 0906.11 for whole Ceylon cinnamon, 0906.20 for ground. Confirm the full code and duty rate in TARIC. See [HS codes for cinnamon](/blog/hs-codes-cinnamon).
- **Preferential origin:** Sri Lanka is a GSP+ beneficiary. Where a duty applies, a statement on origin from a REX-registered exporter may allow preferential treatment. See [REX and certificates of origin](/blog/rex-certificate-origin-gsp).
- **Customs value** based on the transaction value and Incoterm.

## 5. Labelling

Consumer packs must follow Regulation (EU) No 1169/2011: name of the food, net quantity, best-before date, storage conditions, business name and address, country of origin where required, lot number and allergens if applicable. See [labelling cinnamon for the EU, UK and US](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).

## 6. Origin claims and PGI

"Ceylon Cinnamon" is a protected geographical indication in the EU. Use the name and the PGI symbol only as permitted. See [Ceylon cinnamon's PGI status](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-pgi-geographical-indication).

## 7. Documents per shipment

- Commercial invoice and packing list.
- Bill of lading.
- Certificate of origin and/or REX statement.
- Phytosanitary certificate where required.
- Certificate of Analysis for the lot.

See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).

## Summary

EU compliance comes down to three things: safe product backed by lot data, correct customs treatment, and accurate labelling. Build them into your supplier specification from the start.
