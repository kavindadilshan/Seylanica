**Buying Ceylon cinnamon direct from a Sri Lankan exporter usually gives you a better price and clearer traceability; buying through a trader or importer gives you convenience, local stock and less logistics work.** Neither is universally better. The right choice depends on your volumes, capabilities and priorities.

## Side-by-side

| Factor | Direct from origin | Through a trader or importer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price per kg | Lower | Higher (margin and costs) |
| Traceability | Direct line to lots and growers | Depends on the trader |
| Specification control | High | Medium |
| Minimum quantities | Export MOQs | Smaller lots from local stock |
| Lead time | Production plus transit | Often immediate from stock |
| Logistics and customs | Your responsibility (or forwarder) | Handled by trader |
| Credit terms | Usually deposit or L/C | Often open account |
| Currency risk | Yours | Trader's |

## When direct makes sense

- Regular volumes of hundreds of kilos or more per grade.
- You want **specific grades, cuts or packing**.
- **Traceability and origin storytelling** matter to your brand.
- You have, or can build, **import capability** with a forwarder and broker.
- You want to **build a long-term relationship** at origin.

## When a trader makes sense

- Small or irregular volumes.
- You need **fast delivery** from local stock.
- You lack import experience or staff.
- You need **credit terms**.
- You buy **many spices** and value one-stop sourcing.

## A hybrid approach

Many businesses:

1. Start with a local trader to learn the market.
2. **Request samples direct** from origin to benchmark quality and price. See [requesting samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples).
3. Move core grades to **direct sourcing** while keeping a trader for top-ups.

## Making direct sourcing work

- Use a **freight forwarder** experienced in food imports.
- Learn the **documents and compliance** for your market. See [importing into the EU](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-eu) or [the USA](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-usa-fda).
- Write a clear **purchase specification**. See [writing a purchase specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification).
- Plan around **lead times and harvests**. See [planning around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).
