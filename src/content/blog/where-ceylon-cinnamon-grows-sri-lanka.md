**Ceylon cinnamon is grown mainly in Sri Lanka's wet zone, a warm, humid belt along the southern and western coasts, with the districts of Galle, Matara, Kalutara, Hambantota and Ratnapura among the most important.** Climate, soil and generations of peeling skill concentrated in these areas are why Sri Lanka dominates world production of *Cinnamomum verum*.

## The main growing districts

| District | Province | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Galle | Southern | Traditional heartland, many smallholders |
| Matara | Southern | Major producer, strong peeling tradition |
| Hambantota | Southern | Expanding cultivation |
| Kalutara | Western | Long-established gardens |
| Ratnapura | Sabaragamuwa | Inland wet-zone cultivation |

Cinnamon is also grown in smaller quantities in other wet-zone districts.

## Why these regions suit cinnamon

- **Rainfall:** plentiful rain from the monsoons supports vigorous shoot growth and makes bark easier to peel.
- **Warmth:** consistently warm temperatures year-round.
- **Humidity:** supports growth and the peeling process.
- **Soils:** cinnamon grows well on well-drained soils; waterlogging is avoided.
- **Skills:** peeling traditions and communities are concentrated here. See [the peeler's craft](/blog/cinnamon-peeling-craft).

## Smallholder landscape

Much of Sri Lanka's cinnamon is grown on small family holdings, alongside larger estates. Bark moves from growers and peelers through collectors and processors to exporters. See [smallholders in the cinnamon supply chain](/blog/smallholders-cinnamon-supply-chain).

## Why origin within Sri Lanka matters

- **Traceability:** knowing the district and collection point supports food safety and recall readiness. See [garden-to-port traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).
- **Storytelling:** brands can tell a specific, verifiable origin story.
- **Consistency:** working with the same growing areas helps keep quality steady.

## Origin and protection

"Ceylon Cinnamon" is a protected geographical indication in the EU, linking the name to Sri Lankan origin. See [PGI status](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-pgi-geographical-indication).
