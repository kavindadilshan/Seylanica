**A purchase specification is the document that defines exactly what you are buying and how you will decide whether a lot is acceptable.** For Ceylon cinnamon, a good spec covers identity, grade, physical and chemical limits, microbiology, contaminants, packing and documents, each with a clear test method. Use the template below as a starting point.

## Template

### 1. Identity

| Item | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Product | Ceylon cinnamon quills, cut 8 cm |
| Botanical name | *Cinnamomum verum* J.Presl (syn. *C. zeylanicum* Blume) |
| Country of origin | Sri Lanka |
| Grade | C5 Special, SLS 81:2021 |
| HS code | 0906.11 |

See [botanical names](/blog/cinnamomum-verum-vs-zeylanicum-names) and [SLS 81](/blog/sls-81-standard-explained).

### 2. Physical

| Parameter | Limit | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Diameter | Per grade (e.g. about 8 to 10 mm) | Calipers, agreed sampling |
| Length | 8 cm ± 0.5 cm | Ruler |
| Broken pieces | Max agreed % by weight | Weighing |
| Foreign matter | 1% m/m max | ISO 927 |
| Mesh (powder) | Min 95% through 60 mesh | Sieve analysis |

### 3. Chemical

| Parameter | Limit | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Moisture | 12% m/m max (powder 10%) | ISO 939 |
| Volatile oil | Min per grade, e.g. 1.0% v/w | ISO 6571 |
| Total ash | 5% m/m max (powder) | ISO 928 |
| Acid-insoluble ash | 1% m/m max (powder) | ISO 930 |
| Coumarin | Max [your limit] mg/kg | HPLC |
| Sulphur dioxide | Max [your limit] mg/kg | Agreed method |

### 4. Microbiology (powder)

| Organism | Limit | Method |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Total plate count | [your limit] cfu/g | ISO method |
| Yeasts and moulds | [your limit] cfu/g | ISO method |
| *E. coli* | [your limit] cfu/g | ISO method |
| *Salmonella* | Absent in 25 g | ISO method |

Treatment: steam-sterilised / untreated; no irradiation; no ethylene oxide. See [microbiology](/blog/microbiology-testing-spices).

### 5. Contaminants

- Pesticide residues: compliant with [market] MRLs at shipment; accredited multi-residue screen per lot.
- Heavy metals: compliant with [market] limits; Pb, Cd, Hg, As per lot.
- Mycotoxins: per risk-based plan.

### 6. Sensory

Characteristic sweet, floral Ceylon cinnamon aroma and flavour; free from musty, mouldy, smoky or foreign odours. Compare to the approved reference sample.

### 7. Packing and labelling

- Inner: food-grade liner or vacuum bag.
- Outer: carton or bag type, net weight per unit.
- Label: product, grade, lot number, net weight, production date, origin.
- Pallet configuration if required.

### 8. Documents per lot

COA, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate where required, commercial invoice, packing list, B/L. See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).

### 9. Shelf life and storage

Best-before period and storage conditions: cool, dry, away from light and odours. See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

### 10. Acceptance and disputes

Sampling plan, tolerance on retest, referee lab, and remedies.
