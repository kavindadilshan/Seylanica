**Ceylon cinnamon chips, the bark trimmings and pieces not used for quills, are the go-to raw material for steam distillation, solvent extraction and industrial infusion.** They deliver genuine Ceylon chemistry at the lowest cost per kilo, and their value is measured in volatile oil.

## What chips are

Chips include bark trimmings, small pieces and bark too coarse to peel into quills. They are genuine *Cinnamomum verum* bark. See [quillings, featherings and chips](/blog/quillings-featherings-chips).

| Process | Output | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Steam distillation | Cinnamon bark oil | Cinnamaldehyde-rich. See [bark oil vs leaf oil](/blog/cinnamon-bark-oil-vs-leaf-oil) |
| Solvent extraction | Oleoresins, extracts | For flavour houses and food manufacturers |
| Industrial infusion | Syrups, beverage bases | See [infusions and syrups](/blog/infusing-cinnamon-syrups-spirits) |
| Milling | Powder | See [powder mesh](/blog/cinnamon-powder-mesh-size) |

## What drives yield and quality

- **Volatile oil content:** the single most important factor. We specify 0.5% v/w or more typical for chips (ISO 6571). See [volatile oil](/blog/volatile-oil-cinnamon-iso-6571).
- **Particle size:** affects extraction efficiency and steam flow.
- **Moisture:** 12% or less; wet chips risk mould and dilute yield per kilo. See [moisture](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).
- **Cleanliness:** foreign matter and acid-insoluble ash reduce yield and can damage equipment. See [ash](/blog/cinnamon-ash-acid-insoluble-ash).
- **Freshness:** older material loses volatile oil in storage.

## Specifying chips

| Parameter | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Botanical name and origin | *C. verum*, Sri Lanka |
| Form | Chips, size range e.g. 5 to 10 mm |
| Moisture | 12% m/m max (ISO 939) |
| Volatile oil | Minimum per lot (ISO 6571) |
| Foreign matter | 2% m/m max (ISO 927) |
| Packing | 25 kg PP bags with liner, or jumbo bags |

## Buying strategy

- **Price by volatile oil:** compare cost per kilogram of oil, not per kilogram of chips.
- **Test each lot** for volatile oil before processing.
- **Contract supply** for consistent volume. See [annual contracts](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).
- **Store dry and cool** to protect oil content.

## Logistics
