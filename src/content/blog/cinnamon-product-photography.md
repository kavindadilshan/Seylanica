**Good photography is one of the strongest sales tools for Ceylon cinnamon, because the product's key difference, many thin rolled layers, is visible.** Show it clearly, show it in use, and show where it comes from.

## The essential shot list

| Shot | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Pack on white | Marketplace main image; clear and compliant |
| Quill end close-up | Proves Ceylon's layered structure |
| Ceylon vs cassia side by side | Educates and differentiates |
| Powder texture | Shows fineness and colour |
| In use | Stirred in coffee, on a pastry, in a pot |
| Lifestyle | Kitchen, table, gift setting |
| Origin | Plantation, peelers at work, drying racks |
| Scale | Hand holding sticks to show size |

See [how to identify real Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/how-to-identify-real-ceylon-cinnamon).

## Lighting and styling

- **Soft natural light** from a window brings out golden tones.
- **Avoid harsh flash**, which flattens texture.
- **Use neutral props**: wood, linen, ceramics.
- **Keep it real**: accurate colour, no heavy filters.
- **Show detail**: macro shots of layers and texture.

## Origin photography

Images of real growers, peelers and drying racks build trust and support your origin story. Get **consent** from people photographed, and present working conditions honestly. See [the peeler's craft](/blog/cinnamon-peeling-craft) and [sustainable sourcing](/blog/sustainable-cinnamon-sourcing).

## Avoid misleading images

- Do not use stock images of **cassia** for Ceylon products.
- Do not imply **certifications** you do not hold.
- Do not combine with imagery that implies **health benefits**. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).

## SEO for images

- **File names:** `ceylon-cinnamon-alba-sticks-8cm.jpg`, not `IMG_1234.jpg`.
- **Alt text:** describe what is shown: "Close-up of Alba Ceylon cinnamon quill showing thin rolled layers."
- **Compression:** WebP or optimised JPEG for fast loading.
- **Consistency:** same style across website, marketplace and social.

## Video

Short clips of a quill being snapped, powder being sifted, or peelers at work are highly engaging on social media and product pages.

## Summary

Show the layers, show the use, show the origin, and keep every image honest. Well-named, well-described images also help your products appear in image search and AI-generated answers.
