**Selling Ceylon cinnamon on Amazon and other marketplaces works best with a clearly differentiated product, accurate listings, compliant claims and reliable fulfilment.** The category is crowded with cassia, so your job is to make the species and origin difference obvious and credible.

## Before you launch

- **Check category rules.** Food and grocery categories often have additional requirements, approvals or documentation. Read the current seller policies for each marketplace and region.
- **Get labels compliant** for the destination market. See [labelling requirements](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
- **Plan packaging** for fulfilment: drop resistance, leak-proof powder seals, and any marketplace packaging rules. See [packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).
- **Have documents ready:** COAs, certificates of origin and supplier details, in case the marketplace or a customer asks.

## Building the listing

### Title

Include the essentials clearly: brand, "Ceylon Cinnamon", form (sticks or ground), grade if relevant, size, and origin.

### Bullets

- Species: "True cinnamon, *Cinnamomum verum*, from Sri Lanka."
- Grade and format: "Alba grade 8 cm sticks" or "Fine 80 mesh powder."
- Flavour: "Sweet, floral and delicate."
- Uses: baking, drinks, cooking.
- Quality: "Lab-tested lots; traceable lot code on every pack."

### Images

- Hero image of the pack on white.
- Close-up of the layered quill end, which visually proves Ceylon. See [how to identify real Ceylon](/blog/how-to-identify-real-ceylon-cinnamon).
- Ceylon vs cassia comparison graphic, kept factual.
- Lifestyle images in use.
- Origin images. See [product photography](/blog/cinnamon-product-photography).

## Claims to avoid

Marketplaces remove listings with disease or health claims, and regulators act on them too. Avoid references to blood sugar, weight loss, immunity or similar. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).

## Pricing and bundles

- Price against other **Ceylon** listings, not cassia.
- Use **bundles**, such as sticks plus powder, to raise order value.
- Account for marketplace fees and fulfilment costs in your margin. See [retail pricing](/blog/retail-pricing-cinnamon).

## Reviews and quality

- Consistent quality drives reviews; use lot COAs and retained samples to maintain it.
- Respond to questions with factual information.
- Watch for complaints about clumping or weak aroma, which usually point to storage or packaging issues. See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

## Beyond Amazon

Etsy for gifting, specialist food marketplaces, regional platforms, and your own website. See [building a D2C spice brand](/blog/subscription-spice-business).

## Summary

On marketplaces, clarity wins: true cinnamon, clearly explained and consistently delivered. Get compliance and packaging right before launch, and let the layered quill tell the story.
