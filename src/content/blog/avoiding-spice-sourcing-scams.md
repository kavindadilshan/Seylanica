**Online sourcing makes it easy to find spice suppliers, and easy for fraudsters to pose as them.** The most common risks are fake suppliers, payment diversion and product substitution. A few disciplined checks remove most of the risk.

## Common scams

### Fake suppliers

Professional-looking websites and marketplace listings with prices that are too good to be true, asking for full payment upfront.

### Payment diversion

A real supplier's or buyer's email is compromised, and a fraudster sends "updated" bank details just before payment.

### Product substitution

Cassia or blends sold as Ceylon, or lower grades shipped than ordered. See [cinnamon adulteration](/blog/cinnamon-adulteration-detection).

### Document fraud

Fake COAs, certificates or certification logos.

## Verification checklist

- [ ] Company registration and legal name.
- [ ] Physical address and facility, confirmed by video call or visit.
- [ ] Named contacts reachable by phone.
- [ ] Export track record and references.
- [ ] Bank account in the company's legal name, in its country of operation.
- [ ] Certificates verified with the issuing body.
- [ ] Consistent details across website, documents and emails.

## Payment safety

- Use **trade terms** that match the risk: a deposit and balance against documents, or an L/C for new relationships. See [payment terms](/blog/payment-terms-spice-trade).
- **Verify bank details by phone** using a known number.
- Treat **urgent requests** and **changes of account** as red flags.
- Make a **small test payment** for first transactions if appropriate.

## Product safety

- Ask for **samples from the actual lot**. See [requesting samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples).
- Require **lot-specific COAs** and test independently.
- **Check on arrival** against your retained sample.
- For high-value orders, consider **pre-shipment inspection**.

## Red flags

| Red flag | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Price far below market | Likely fraud or substitution |
| Full prepayment demanded, no exceptions | No recourse if goods never ship |
| No samples possible | Nothing to verify |
| Bank account in another country or name | Payment diversion risk |
| Generic stock photos only | No proof of operation |
| Pressure to decide quickly | Classic fraud tactic |
