**Importing Ceylon cinnamon into Great Britain means following UK customs, UK tariff and GB food rules, which have developed separately from the EU's since Brexit.** For most food businesses the principles are familiar, but the details, from tariff preferences to pesticide limits, need to be checked against UK sources.

## 1. Registrations

- **GB EORI number.**
- **Food business registration** with your local authority, if you operate a food business in the UK.
- **Food safety management** based on HACCP principles.

## 2. Customs and tariffs

- **Classification:** commodity codes under heading 0906; 0906.11 for whole Ceylon cinnamon and 0906.20 for ground. See [HS codes for cinnamon](/blog/hs-codes-cinnamon).
- **UK Global Tariff:** check the current rate for your code.
- **Preferences:** Sri Lanka is covered by the **Developing Countries Trading Scheme (DCTS)**, which can reduce or remove duties where they apply, if origin rules are met and documented. See [certificates of origin and preferential tariffs](/blog/rex-certificate-origin-gsp).
- **Customs declarations** are usually filed by a broker or freight forwarder.

## 3. Plant health

Check whether your product form requires a phytosanitary certificate and pre-notification under GB plant health rules. Requirements depend on the commodity. See [the phytosanitary certificate](/blog/phytosanitary-certificate-npqs).

## 4. Food safety rules

| Area | What to check |
| --- | --- |
| Pesticides | GB MRLs (may differ from EU). See [pesticide residues](/blog/pesticide-residues-eu-mrl-cinnamon) |
| Contaminants | GB contaminant limits for spices. See [heavy metals](/blog/heavy-metals-spices-testing) |
| Flavourings | Coumarin limits in finished foods under retained flavourings law. See [coumarin](/blog/coumarin-in-cinnamon-explained) |
| Microbiology | Buyer specs; Salmonella absent in 25 g |
| Treatments | Irradiation must be labelled; ethylene oxide is not permitted |

## 5. Labelling

Retail labels in Great Britain must meet UK food information rules, which are based on retained EU law: name, net quantity, best-before, storage, business name and UK address, lot number and allergens. If you sell in both GB and the EU, you may need address details for both markets. See [labelling cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).

## 6. Northern Ireland

Northern Ireland operates under separate arrangements with links to EU rules for goods. If you supply NI, check the applicable regime.

## 7. Documents per shipment

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, origin documentation, phytosanitary certificate where required, and lot COA. See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).

## Summary

The UK market values Ceylon cinnamon and offers preferential access for Sri Lankan origin, but UK rules are now their own.
