**Organic Ceylon cinnamon is cinnamon produced, processed and handled under a recognised organic standard and certified by an approved control body for the market where it is sold.** For buyers, "organic" is a legal claim, so certification and documentation matter as much as the product itself.

## The main organic regimes

| Market | Framework | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU | Regulation (EU) 2018/848 | Imports need a Certificate of Inspection (COI) in TRACES |
| UK | GB organic rules | Separate import controls and certificates |
| US | USDA National Organic Program (NOP) | Certification by USDA-accredited certifiers; import certificates |

Rules change; check current requirements with your certifier.

## What certification covers

- **Production:** no synthetic pesticides or fertilisers, with approved inputs only.
- **Processing and handling:** segregation from non-organic product, approved cleaning and treatments.
- **Records:** traceability from farm to sale.
- **Inspection:** annual inspections and risk-based sampling.

## Import documentation (EU example)

1. The exporter's certification covers the product.
2. A **Certificate of Inspection** is issued electronically in **TRACES** for each consignment.
3. The consignment is verified on arrival by the competent authority.
4. The importer must also be certified.

## Testing still matters

Organic certification governs methods, not outcomes. Drift, contamination or fraud can still occur, so test organic lots for residues and contaminants. See [pesticide residues](/blog/pesticide-residues-eu-mrl-cinnamon) and [heavy metals](/blog/heavy-metals-spices-testing).

## Treatments

- **Irradiation:** not permitted under organic rules.
- **Ethylene oxide:** not permitted.
- **Steam sterilisation:** generally used for organic powders. See [steam vs irradiation vs EtO](/blog/steam-sterilisation-vs-irradiation-spices).

## Buying considerations

- **Price:** organic carries a premium for certification and lower yields.
- **Availability:** certified volumes are smaller; plan ahead.
- **Chain of custody:** every handler, including your packer, may need certification.
- **Labelling:** use logos and wording only as permitted. See [labelling](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
