It surprises people that one of the Ceylon cinnamon grades is named after a country on the other side of the world. It makes sense once you know that Mexico has been one of Sri Lanka's biggest cinnamon customers for a very long time. *Canela* is part of daily life there, and Mexican buyers wanted a particular kind of stick. The trade obliged and named it after them.

## Where they sit

SLS 81 groups quills into four families by thickness: Alba, Continental, Mexican and Hamburg. Mexican grades come third. They're sturdier than C5 or C4 but finer than the Hamburg grades used mainly for milling. As in the continental family, M5 is the finer of the two.

| Family | Relative thickness | Typical use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Alba | Thinnest | Luxury retail |
| Continental (C5 Special to C3) | Thin to medium | Retail, import, manufacturing |
| **Mexican (M5, M4)** | **Medium-thick** | **Simmered drinks, desserts, home cooking** |
| Hamburg (H1 to H3) | Thickest | Grinding and extraction |

## Why the Mexican market liked them

Think about how cinnamon gets used in a Mexican kitchen. It goes into hot chocolate, *café de olla*, *atole*, *arroz con leche*, all sorts of syrups and sweets. Very often a whole stick is simmered in milk or water for a good while and then fished out. A thin, fragile Alba quill would break apart; a sturdy medium-thick stick holds together and gives up its flavour steadily. Ceylon's sweet, floral profile also suits all that milk and chocolate far better than harsh cassia would.

## Where we'd use them

- Simmered drinks: hot chocolate, spiced coffee, rice and nut milks.
- Rice puddings, custards and other milk-based desserts.
- Syrups, preserves and poaching liquids.
- Home-cooking retail packs in markets where shoppers expect a solid stick.

## Writing them into a spec

Same principles as any grade. Name the grade and the SLS 81 edition, the length (42 cm or cut), a moisture limit by ISO 939, a minimum volatile oil by ISO 6571, a foreign matter limit by ISO 927, and the packing you want, whether cartons or pressed bales. Our template in [writing a purchase specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification) works for Mexican grades too.

## Getting them to Latin America

Ceylon cinnamon reaches Latin American ports by sea from Colombo, usually on CFR or CIF terms and often via a transhipment port. Allow for that in your planning. There's more in [sea vs air freight](/blog/shipping-cinnamon-sea-vs-air).
