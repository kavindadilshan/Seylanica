**The price of Ceylon cinnamon is driven mainly by grade, then by harvest and market conditions at origin, and finally by the costs of testing, packing and shipping to you.** Bulk prices are usually quoted per inquiry for that reason. Understanding the drivers helps you compare quotes properly and buy at the right time.

## 1. Grade

Grade is the biggest single factor. From most to least expensive, roughly:

Alba > C5 Special > C5 > C4 > Mexican and Hamburg grades > quillings and featherings > chips

Finer grades take more skill and are scarcer. See [grades explained](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-grades-guide) and [why Ceylon costs more](/blog/why-ceylon-cinnamon-costs-more).

## 2. Harvest and supply

- **Seasonality:** cinnamon has main harvest periods tied to the monsoons. See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season).
- **Weather:** heavy or insufficient rain affects peeling and drying. See [weather, climate and supply](/blog/climate-impact-cinnamon-supply).
- **Labour:** availability and cost of skilled peelers. See [the skills gap](/blog/cinnamon-skills-training-peelers).

## 3. Demand

Global demand from major importing markets, and trends such as clean-label and origin-led products, influence prices. See [market trends](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-market-trends).

## 4. Exchange rates

Origin prices are linked to the Sri Lankan rupee, while export quotes are usually in USD or EUR. Currency moves can shift prices in either direction.

## 5. Specification and testing

Tighter specs, such as lower moisture, higher volatile oil or steam-sterilised powder, and more extensive testing all add cost. They also reduce risk. See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).

## 6. Packing

Vacuum packing, retail packs, private labels and pressed bales each carry different costs. See [packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).

## 7. Volume

Larger orders spread fixed costs. Prices typically step down at volume thresholds and container loads. See [MOQs explained](/blog/moq-ceylon-cinnamon).

## 8. Incoterm and freight

FOB, CFR, CIF and DAP prices include different costs. Freight rates themselves fluctuate. See [Incoterms for spice importers](/blog/incoterms-for-spice-importers).

## Comparing quotes properly

| Check | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Same grade and spec | Avoid comparing C5 with C4 |
| Same Incoterm and place | FOB and CIF are not comparable |
| Same packing | Vacuum cartons cost more than bags |
| Same testing scope | Full COA vs basic tests |
| Validity date | Prices move |
| Payment terms | Credit has a cost |

Calculate **landed cost per kilo** and, for flavour-driven uses, **cost per unit of volatile oil**.

## Summary

Price follows grade first, then harvest, demand and currency, then the specifics of your order. Compare quotes on a like-for-like basis and plan purchases with the harvest calendar. [Request a quote](/quote) with your grade, volume, Incoterm and port for a precise price, usually within 24 hours.
