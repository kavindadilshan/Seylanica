**Managing spice supply chain risk means identifying what could go wrong, from contamination to a missed shipment, and putting proportionate controls in place before it happens.** For Ceylon cinnamon buyers, a simple risk register covers most of what matters.

## The main risk categories

| Risk | Examples | Key controls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quality | Wrong grade, low aroma, moisture | Spec, samples, COA, arrival checks. See [writing a specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification) |
| Food safety | Salmonella, residues, metals | Lot testing, treated powder. See [microbiology](/blog/microbiology-testing-spices) |
| Fraud | Cassia substitution | Traceability, coumarin tests. See [adulteration](/blog/cinnamon-adulteration-detection) |
| Supply | Poor harvest, labour shortages | Contracts, safety stock. See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season) |
| Logistics | Delays, condensation damage | Forwarder, packing, buffers. See [container loading](/blog/container-loading-cinnamon) |
| Price | Spikes, currency | Contracts, reviews. See [price volatility](/blog/cinnamon-price-volatility-risk) |
| Compliance | Labelling, claims, documents | Reviews, templates. See [labelling](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us) |
| Payment | Fraud, disputes | Verified bank details, trade terms. See [avoiding fraud](/blog/avoiding-spice-sourcing-scams) |
| Reputation | Recalls, misleading claims | All of the above |

## Building a risk register

For each risk, record:

1. **Description.**
2. **Likelihood** (low, medium, high).
3. **Impact** (low, medium, high).
4. **Controls** in place.
5. **Owner.**
6. **Review date.**

Focus effort on high-likelihood, high-impact risks first.

## Core controls

- **Specifications** agreed with suppliers.
- **Lot COAs** reviewed before shipment. See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).
- **Traceability** from origin to your packs. See [traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).
- **Supplier approval and audits.** See [audit checklist](/blog/supplier-audit-checklist-spices).
- **Arrival testing** on a risk basis.
- **Safety stock** and **backup suppliers**.
- **Contracts** for volume and price.
- **Insurance** for cargo and product liability.

## Incident response

Have a simple plan for:

- Holding and investigating suspect lots.
- Notifying customers and authorities where required.
- Withdrawing or recalling products.
- Root-cause analysis and corrective action.

## Review

Review the register at least annually, and after any incident, new product, new supplier or new market.
