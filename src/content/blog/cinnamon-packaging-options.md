**The best cinnamon packaging protects aroma and texture while selling the product on shelf.** The main enemies are moisture, light, air, foreign odours and crushing. Different formats balance those against cost, appearance and sustainability.

## Comparison

| Packaging | Best for | Barrier | Shelf appeal | Cost | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Glass jar | Sticks, powder | Good (if dark or boxed) | High | Medium | Heavy; light exposure if clear |
| Tin | Sticks, powder, gifts | Very good | High | Medium-high | Great for gifting |
| Paper or plastic tube | Sticks | Varies | High | Low-medium | Shows off long sticks |
| Laminated pouch (with zip) | Powder, sticks | Very good | Medium-high | Low | Lightweight, efficient shipping |
| Kraft pouch with liner | Powder, sticks | Good | Natural look | Low | Check liner barrier |
| Sachet or stick pack | Single servings, food service | Good | Functional | Low | Portion control |

## Sticks vs powder

- **Sticks** keep aroma longer because less surface is exposed. Main risks: breakage and moisture. Tubes and rigid jars help.
- **Powder** loses aroma faster and can cake. It needs a stronger barrier and a reliable reseal. See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

## Design considerations

- **Window or clear jar:** shows the product, but light degrades aroma; consider UV-protective materials or limited windows.
- **Reseal:** zip pouches, tight lids or shaker-sifter caps for powder.
- **Fit:** test stick length against tube or jar height. See [cut stick formats](/blog/cut-cinnamon-sticks-sizes).
- **Label space:** leave room for mandatory information. See [labelling requirements](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
- **Food contact compliance:** confirm materials meet your market's rules.

## Sustainability

- **Glass and tins** are widely recyclable and refillable.
- **Mono-material pouches** can be recyclable where infrastructure exists.
- **Paper tubes** with suitable liners reduce plastic.
- Lighter packs reduce transport emissions.

Validate shelf life in any new material before switching.
