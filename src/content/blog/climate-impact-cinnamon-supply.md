**Ceylon cinnamon supply is closely tied to the weather: rain makes the bark peelable, dry spells slow peeling, and heavy rain during drying threatens quality.** As weather patterns become less predictable, buyers who understand these links plan better and suffer fewer surprises.

## How weather affects each stage

| Stage | Weather effect |
| --- | --- |
| Growth | Rainfall and warmth drive shoot growth |
| Peeling | Rain-driven sap flow makes bark peel cleanly; drought slows peeling |
| Drying | Persistent humidity and rain slow drying and raise mould risk |
| Storage | High humidity can raise moisture in stored product |
| Logistics | Storms and floods can disrupt transport |

See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season) and [drying and processing](/blog/cinnamon-drying-processing).

## Climate variability

Sri Lanka's rainfall depends on its two monsoons. Shifts in timing, intensity and dry spells affect:

- **When** peeling peaks occur.
- **How much** bark is peeled.
- **Quality**, through drying conditions.

## Quality risks to watch

- **Higher moisture** in lots dried in wet weather. See [moisture](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).
- **Mould** and, in extreme cases, mycotoxin risk. See [mycotoxins](/blog/mycotoxins-aflatoxin-ochratoxin-spices).
- **Colour variation** and foxing.

Lot COAs are your early warning system.

## Building resilience as a buyer

1. **Share forecasts** early so suppliers can secure material.
2. **Contract base volumes**. See [annual contracts](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).
3. **Hold safety stock**. See [planning around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).
4. **Qualify alternatives**, such as an adjacent grade or a second supplier.
5. **Tighten incoming checks** in difficult seasons.
6. **Stay in touch** with your supplier about conditions on the ground.

## At origin

Growers and processors adapt through:

- Improved drying facilities that are less weather-dependent.
- Better storage with moisture control.
- Soil and water management in gardens.
- Diversified income for smallholders.

See [sustainable sourcing](/blog/sustainable-cinnamon-sourcing).
