**The Port of Colombo is Sri Lanka's main container port and one of South Asia's busiest transhipment hubs, which gives cinnamon exporters frequent sailings to most of the world.** If you buy FOB Colombo or simply want to understand the export leg, here is how cinnamon moves from warehouse to ship.

## Why Colombo matters

- **Location:** close to the main east-west shipping lanes between Asia, the Middle East and Europe.
- **Transhipment hub:** much of its traffic is cargo moving between larger and smaller vessels, which means many service options.
- **Proximity to growing areas:** Sri Lanka's main cinnamon districts in the south and west are within road distance of Colombo. See [where Ceylon cinnamon grows](/blog/where-ceylon-cinnamon-grows-sri-lanka).

## From warehouse to vessel

1. **Order confirmed** and lot allocated; COA prepared. See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).
2. **Packing and labelling** with lot numbers.
3. **Booking:** under FOB, your forwarder books the vessel; under CFR/CIF, the seller books.
4. **Container release** and **stuffing** at the exporter's facility or a container freight station.
5. **Phytosanitary inspection** by NPQS where required. See [the phytosanitary certificate](/blog/phytosanitary-certificate-npqs).
6. **Export customs clearance.**
7. **Delivery to terminal** before the cut-off.
8. **Loading** and **bill of lading** issued.

## FOB Colombo responsibilities

| Seller | Buyer |
| --- | --- |
| Export packing and marking | Nominating forwarder and vessel |
| Export clearance and documents | Ocean freight |
| Delivery to the terminal and loading on board | Insurance from loading |
| Origin, quality and phyto certificates | Import clearance and onward delivery |

See [Incoterms for spice importers](/blog/incoterms-for-spice-importers).

## Planning tips

- **Book early** in peak seasons; space can tighten.
- **Allow time** for inspection and documents before the cut-off.
- **Share forwarder contacts** with your supplier at order confirmation.
- **Check transhipment ports** on your route; they affect transit time and risk.
- **Avoid long dwell times** in hot terminals to reduce condensation risk. See [container loading](/blog/container-loading-cinnamon).

## Typical timelines

From a confirmed order and deposit, our lead time is generally two to five weeks to shipment depending on grade and volume, then port-to-port transit. See [planning purchases around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).

## Summary

Colombo's connectivity is an advantage for Ceylon cinnamon buyers: frequent sailings and flexible routing. Coordinate early with your supplier and forwarder, respect the cut-off, and keep documents ready.
