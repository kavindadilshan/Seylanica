**Ceylon cinnamon is cultivated as a coppiced bush: plants are cut back regularly so that many straight young shoots grow from the base, and those shoots are harvested for their bark.** This system, developed over centuries in Sri Lanka, produces the smooth, peelable bark that hand-rolled quills require.

## From planting to harvest

1. **Planting:** seedlings or cuttings are planted, often in small clumps.
2. **Establishment:** plants grow for a few years before the first harvest.
3. **First cut:** stems are cut close to the ground to start the coppice.
4. **Regrowth:** several new shoots grow from the stump.
5. **Repeated harvests:** mature shoots are harvested every couple of years, leaving the rootstock to regrow.

A well-managed garden can produce for many years.

## Why coppicing works

- Produces **straight, uniform shoots**.
- Keeps bark **thin and smooth**, ideal for peeling.
- Allows **regular harvests** from the same plants.
- Keeps bushes at a **manageable height** for workers.

## Climate and soil

| Factor | Preference |
| --- | --- |
| Temperature | Warm year-round |
| Rainfall | Plentiful, well distributed |
| Humidity | High |
| Soil | Well drained; waterlogging avoided |
| Elevation | Mainly low country |

These conditions are found in Sri Lanka's wet zone. See [where Ceylon cinnamon grows](/blog/where-ceylon-cinnamon-grows-sri-lanka).

## Field practices that affect quality

- **Shoot management:** removing weak shoots focuses growth.
- **Weed control:** reduces competition.
- **Soil fertility:** organic matter and balanced nutrition.
- **Input use:** following Good Agricultural Practices keeps residues within limits. See [pesticide residues](/blog/pesticide-residues-eu-mrl-cinnamon).
- **Harvest timing:** cutting when bark peels well. See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season).

## Sustainability considerations

- **Soil conservation** on slopes.
- **Intercropping** and shade trees in some systems.
- **Leaf and twig use** for distillation reduces waste. See [by-products](/blog/cinnamon-by-products-zero-waste).
- **Fair returns** to growers keep gardens productive. See [smallholders](/blog/smallholders-cinnamon-supply-chain).
