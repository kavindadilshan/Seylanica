**A successful first cinnamon import follows a simple sequence: define what you need, test samples, place a trial order, organise logistics and compliance, then check the goods on arrival.** Here is each step, with links to deeper guides.

## Step 1: Define your requirements

- **Product and grade:** quills, cut sticks, chips or powder; which grade. See [grades explained](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-grades-guide).
- **Volume:** first order and expected annual volume.
- **Destination:** country and port.
- **Compliance:** your market's rules. See [EU](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-eu), [USA](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-usa-fda), [UK](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-uk).
- **Packing and labelling:** bulk or retail, private label.

## Step 2: Shortlist suppliers

Ask evidence-based questions. See [how to choose a supplier](/blog/how-to-choose-ceylon-cinnamon-supplier).

## Step 3: Samples and specification

Request samples from the lot on offer with its COA, test them in your application, and agree a specification. See [requesting samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples) and [writing a specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification).

## Step 4: Quotation

Ask for a quote stating: product, grade, spec, quantity, packing, Incoterm and place, price, validity, lead time, payment terms and documents. See [what determines price](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-price-factors).

## Step 5: Logistics and import set-up

- Appoint a **freight forwarder** (essential under FOB).
- Appoint a **customs broker**.
- Get your **importer registrations** in place (EORI, FDA-related steps, licences).
- Confirm **duty and classification**. See [HS codes](/blog/hs-codes-cinnamon).

## Step 6: Order and payment

Issue a purchase order referencing the spec. Pay the deposit or open the L/C. See [payment terms](/blog/payment-terms-spice-trade). Verify bank details by phone.

## Step 7: Pre-shipment

- Receive the **lot COA** and approve it.
- Optionally request **pre-shipment photos** or an inspection.
- Check **draft documents** for consistency. See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).

## Step 8: Shipping and tracking

Track the vessel and share documents with your broker in advance. See [sea vs air freight](/blog/shipping-cinnamon-sea-vs-air).

## Step 9: Customs clearance

Your broker files the declaration with the invoice, packing list, B/L, origin and other certificates.

## Step 10: Arrival checks

- Check seals, packaging and condition.
- Measure moisture and compare with the COA.
- Compare against your **retained sample**.
- Test as per your QA plan.
- Record lot numbers for traceability.

## Step 11: Review

Score the supplier on quality, documents, timing and communication before the next order.

## Summary

Start small, test thoroughly and set up logistics early, and your first import becomes the template for every order that follows. [Request samples](/quote?type=sample) to begin, and use our [Buyer Hub](/buyer-hub) for spec sheets and a shipping estimate.
