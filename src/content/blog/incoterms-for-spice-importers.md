**Incoterms are the standard trade terms that decide who pays for each part of the journey and at what point risk passes from seller to buyer.** For Ceylon cinnamon shipped from Colombo, the most common are FOB, CFR and CIF for sea freight, and DAP for air shipments of samples and small orders. Here is how to choose.

## The four terms at a glance

| Term | Seller arranges and pays | Risk passes to buyer | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **FOB** Colombo | Export clearance, delivery on board | When goods are on board the vessel at Colombo | Importers with their own forwarder |
| **CFR** destination port | FOB plus main sea freight | On board at Colombo | Simpler buying, buyer insures |
| **CIF** destination port | CFR plus minimum insurance | On board at Colombo | First-time importers |
| **DAP** named place | All transport to your named place, not import duties | On arrival at named place, ready for unloading | Air samples, small orders |

## FOB (Free On Board)

The seller clears the goods for export and loads them on the vessel you nominate at Colombo. You pay freight, insurance and everything after.

- **Pros:** control over freight rates, carrier and routing; can consolidate with other cargo.
- **Cons:** you need a reliable forwarder and must coordinate with the seller.

## CFR (Cost and Freight)

As FOB, but the seller also books and pays sea freight to your port.

- **Pros:** one price to your port; fewer moving parts.
- **Cons:** risk still passes at loading, so arrange your own insurance; less control of carrier.

## CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight)

As CFR, plus the seller buys cargo insurance. Under Incoterms 2020, CIF requires only **minimum cover (Institute Cargo Clauses C)**. If you want all-risks cover, ask for it explicitly or insure yourself.

## DAP (Delivered at Place)

The seller delivers to your named place, typically by air courier for samples or by air freight for urgent small orders. The buyer handles import clearance and duties unless agreed otherwise.

## Common mistakes

- **Using FOB for air or courier shipments.** FOB, CFR and CIF are for sea and inland waterway transport only. Use FCA, CPT, CIP or DAP for air and containers handed over at a terminal, where appropriate.
- **Assuming CIF insurance is comprehensive.**
- **Leaving the place vague.** Always name it: "FOB Colombo, Incoterms 2020".
- **Forgetting destination charges.** Terminal handling, customs clearance and delivery at destination are usually for the buyer under CFR and CIF.

## Which should you choose?

- **New importer, small volumes:** CIF or CFR.
- **Experienced importer with a forwarder:** FOB.
- **Samples and urgent air orders:** DAP.
