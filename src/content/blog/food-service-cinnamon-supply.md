**Food service is a large and loyal market for Ceylon cinnamon: cafés, bakeries, hotels and restaurants value its delicate flavour and the menu appeal of "true cinnamon".** Winning it requires the right formats, consistency and a reliable supply route.

## What food service buyers want

- **Consistent flavour** from batch to batch.
- **Practical pack sizes** for their usage.
- **Reliable supply** with short reorder times.
- **Clear pricing** and simple ordering.
- **Documentation** for their food safety files.

## Formats and pack sizes

| Outlet | Format | Pack |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Café | 80 mesh powder for dusting, cut sticks for drinks | 500 g to 1 kg bags, shaker refills, 5 to 8 cm sticks |
| Bakery | 60 mesh powder | 1 kg to 25 kg bags |
| Hotel and restaurant | Powder, sticks, whole quills | 1 kg packs, mixed |
| Bar | Cut sticks, syrups | Sticks for garnish, quills for infusion |
| Catering | Powder and C4 quills | 1 kg trade packs |

See [cut stick formats](/blog/cut-cinnamon-sticks-sizes) and [powder mesh sizes](/blog/cinnamon-powder-mesh-size).

## Grades for food service

- **C5 Special or Alba** sticks where the stick is served.
- **C4 or C5** for infusion and cooking. See [C4 cinnamon](/blog/c4-cinnamon-grade-uses).
- **Powder** for baking and dusting.

## Menu messaging

"Ceylon cinnamon" on a menu signals quality. Examples:

- "Ceylon cinnamon latte, with true cinnamon from Sri Lanka."
- "Cinnamon buns made with Ceylon cinnamon."

Keep claims to flavour and origin. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).

## Routes to market

- **Food service distributors and wholesalers:** reach many outlets efficiently.
- **Direct to chains:** larger volumes, tighter specs and audits.
- **Cash-and-carry:** 1 kg trade packs.
- **Online trade accounts** for independents.

## Documentation

Food service buyers increasingly ask for:

- Spec sheets and COAs.
- Allergen statements.
- Supplier approval questionnaires.

See [how to read a spec sheet](/blog/how-to-read-cinnamon-spec-sheet).

## Pricing

Food service is price-aware but values consistency. Offer clear tiers by pack and volume, and show cost per serving to make Ceylon's premium tangible. See [retail pricing](/blog/retail-pricing-cinnamon).

## Summary

Food service rewards suppliers who are consistent, convenient and well documented. Offer practical formats, back menu claims with genuine supply, and work through distributors to scale.
