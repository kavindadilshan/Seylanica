**A successful spice import business starts narrow: one strong product, one clear customer group, solid compliance and reliable supply.** Ceylon cinnamon is an excellent anchor product, premium, differentiated and widely used. Here is a practical roadmap.

## Phase 1: Choose your niche

| Niche | Customers | Products |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bakery and manufacturing | Bakeries, cereal and snack makers | Powder, specs, coumarin data |
| Retail brand | Shoppers via retail and online | Cut sticks, powder, gift sets |
| Food service | Cafés, restaurants, hotels | 1 kg packs, sticks |
| Tea and beverage | Blenders, cafés | Tea-cut, sticks |
| Wholesale | Smaller importers and packers | Bulk quills and chips |

See [Ceylon cinnamon market trends](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-market-trends).

## Phase 2: Legal and compliance foundations

- Company registration, tax and bank account.
- Importer registrations (EORI, FDA-related steps, licences as applicable).
- Food business registration and a HACCP-based food safety plan.
- Product liability insurance.
- Understanding of labelling and claims rules.

See [importing into the EU](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-eu), [USA](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-usa-fda) and [UK](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-uk).

## Phase 3: Suppliers

- Shortlist with evidence-based questions. See [choosing a supplier](/blog/how-to-choose-ceylon-cinnamon-supplier).
- Test samples. See [requesting samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples).
- Agree a specification. See [writing a specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification).

## Phase 4: Logistics

- Choose a freight forwarder and customs broker.
- Decide Incoterms. See [Incoterms](/blog/incoterms-for-spice-importers).
- Arrange food-grade storage.

## Phase 5: First order

Start with a trial order. See [your first import order](/blog/first-cinnamon-import-order-guide).

## Phase 6: Sales and marketing

- **B2B:** samples, spec sheets, trade fairs, direct outreach. See [B2B marketing](/blog/b2b-marketing-spice-exporters).
- **B2C:** brand, packaging, marketplaces, content. See [D2C brands](/blog/subscription-spice-business).
- **Content:** helpful articles build trust and search visibility.

## Phase 7: Finance

| Cash outflow | Timing |
| --- | --- |
| Deposit | At order |
| Balance | Against documents |
| Freight, insurance | Before or at shipment |
| Duties, clearance | On arrival |
| Testing, storage | On arrival |
| Packaging, marketing | Before sales |

Plan for a gap of months between paying for stock and receiving payment from customers.

## Phase 8: Scale

Add grades and formats, then adjacent spices, once your core product is profitable and your systems are solid.
