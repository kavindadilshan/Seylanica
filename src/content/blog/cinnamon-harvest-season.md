**Ceylon cinnamon peeling follows the rain: shoots are cut and peeled mainly during and after the monsoon periods, when sap flow makes the bark easy to separate.** Sri Lanka's two monsoons create two main peeling periods each year, and buyers who plan around them get better availability.

## Why rain matters

After rain, cinnamon shoots are full of sap. The inner bark separates from the wood cleanly, allowing peelers to lift long, thin strips without tearing. In dry conditions, the bark sticks, peeling slows and quality suffers.

## Two monsoons, two main seasons

Sri Lanka has two main monsoon systems:

- **Southwest monsoon**, bringing heavy rain to the southwest wet zone roughly in the middle of the year.
- **Northeast monsoon**, bringing rain later in the year and into the early months of the next.

Cinnamon peeling activity tends to peak in and after these rainy periods. Exact timing varies by year and district, and climate variability is making patterns less predictable. See [weather, climate and cinnamon supply](/blog/climate-impact-cinnamon-supply).

## What it means for buyers

| Period | Supply picture | Buyer action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| During and just after peeling peaks | Fresh material, wider grade availability | Place larger and annual orders |
| Between peaks | Stocks drawn down; fine grades can tighten | Rely on safety stock and contracted volumes |
| Unusual weather | Possible delays and quality variation | Check COAs closely; plan buffers |

## Planning tips

1. **Share forecasts** with your supplier so they can secure material during peaks.
2. **Contract base volumes** for the year. See [annual contracts vs spot buying](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).
3. **Hold safety stock** to bridge gaps. See [planning around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).
4. **Book early** for festive-season demand.

## Freshness and storage

Well-dried quills store well, so cinnamon peeled in one season can serve demand for months if stored properly. Volatile oil results on each lot's COA show how aromatic the material is. See [volatile oil](/blog/volatile-oil-cinnamon-iso-6571).
