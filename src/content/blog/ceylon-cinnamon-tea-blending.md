**Tea blenders use Ceylon cinnamon mainly as tea-cut, small, evenly sized and dust-extracted pieces, for its sweet, floral flavour that complements tea rather than overpowering it.** Getting the cut, cleanliness and consistency right is as important as the cinnamon itself.

## Why Ceylon suits tea

- **Sweet and floral** rather than hot, so it blends with delicate teas.
- **Natural sweetness perception** in unsweetened blends.
- **Origin pairing:** Ceylon cinnamon with Ceylon tea tells a single-origin story.

## Formats for tea

| Format | Use | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tea-cut (about 3 to 5 mm) | Tea bags, loose blends | Dust-extracted, even size |
| Larger chips (5 to 10 mm) | Loose-leaf blends, chai | Visible pieces |
| Fine powder | Instant mixes, some bags | Disperses fast; can cloud |
| Cut sticks | Premium loose tea, gift sets | Visual appeal |

See [quillings, featherings and chips](/blog/quillings-featherings-chips).

## Blend ideas

- **Ceylon black tea with cinnamon:** a classic single-origin pairing.
- **Masala chai:** cinnamon with cardamom, ginger, clove and black pepper.
- **Rooibos cinnamon:** naturally caffeine-free.
- **Apple cinnamon herbal:** dried apple, cinnamon, hibiscus.
- **Festive blends:** orange peel, clove, cinnamon.

## Quality control for tea-cut

- **Sieve analysis:** specify the size range and maximum fines.
- **Dust extraction:** essential for tea bags.
- **Foreign matter and ash:** low limits; tea drinkers notice grit. See [ash in cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-ash-acid-insoluble-ash).
- **Moisture:** 12% or less to prevent mould in blends. See [moisture](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).
- **Volatile oil:** consistent aroma per lot. See [volatile oil](/blog/volatile-oil-cinnamon-iso-6571).
- **Microbiology:** many tea buyers set micro limits on botanicals, since water temperatures vary. See [microbiological testing](/blog/microbiology-testing-spices).
- **Pesticide residues:** tea markets are strict. See [pesticide residues](/blog/pesticide-residues-eu-mrl-cinnamon).

## Blending tips

- **Pre-blend** cinnamon with a carrier to improve distribution in large batches.
- **Check segregation:** heavier pieces settle; test bag-to-bag consistency.
- **Protect aroma:** pack blends in barrier materials. See [packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).

## Labelling

Name the cinnamon accurately ("Ceylon cinnamon") in the ingredients list where the supply supports it, and avoid health claims. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).

## Summary

For tea, cut size, cleanliness and consistency matter as much as flavour. Specify tea-cut size, dust extraction and micro limits, and Ceylon cinnamon will lift any blend.
