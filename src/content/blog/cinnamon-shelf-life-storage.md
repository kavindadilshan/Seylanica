**Dry cinnamon is a stable product, but it does not last forever: its aroma, carried by volatile oils, gradually fades, and powder fades faster than whole sticks.** The best-before date on your pack should reflect when quality, mostly aroma, drops below your standard, based on testing in your packaging.

## What causes deterioration

| Factor | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Time | Gradual loss of volatile oils |
| Heat | Accelerates aroma loss |
| Light | Degrades aroma compounds and colour |
| Air (oxygen) | Oxidation and aroma loss |
| Moisture | Caking (powder), mould risk |
| Odours | Cinnamon absorbs foreign smells |
| Insects | Infestation in poor storage |

## Sticks vs powder

- **Whole sticks** have less exposed surface, so they keep aroma longer.
- **Powder** has a huge surface area and loses aroma faster. It also cakes if moisture gets in.

That is why many buyers store quills and grind closer to use.

## Storage recommendations

**Warehouse:**

- Cool, dry and ventilated; avoid direct sun and heat sources.
- Off the floor on pallets, away from walls.
- Away from strong-smelling goods and chemicals.
- Pest control programme.
- First-expiry, first-out rotation.

**Retail and home:**

- Airtight container, away from the stove and sunlight.
- Close packs tightly after use.

## Setting best-before dates

1. **Define quality limits:** for example, minimum volatile oil, maximum moisture, sensory score, and micro limits.
2. **Store samples** in your final packaging at normal and accelerated conditions.
3. **Test at intervals:** volatile oil, moisture, sensory and, for powder, microbiology.
4. **Set the date** before quality drops below your limits, with a margin.
5. **Review** when you change packaging or supplier.

See [volatile oil explained](/blog/volatile-oil-cinnamon-iso-6571) and [moisture in cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).

## Packaging that extends shelf life

- High moisture and oxygen barrier films or tins.
- Low light exposure.
- Good reseal for repeat use.
- Nitrogen flushing or vacuum packing in some formats.

See [cinnamon packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).

## Label wording

- "Best before: [date]. Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Reseal after opening."

## Summary

Cinnamon's shelf life is about aroma. Protect it with barrier packaging and good storage, and set best-before dates from your own tests.
