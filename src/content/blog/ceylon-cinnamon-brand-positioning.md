**The strongest positioning for a Ceylon cinnamon brand is built on facts customers can verify: it is a different species, from a specific origin, hand-made and graded, with a distinctly delicate flavour.** That is premium enough without resorting to vague superlatives or health claims that can get a brand into trouble.

## Your credible differentiators

| Differentiator | How to express it |
| --- | --- |
| Species | "True cinnamon, *Cinnamomum verum*" |
| Origin | "Grown and hand-rolled in Sri Lanka" |
| Craft | "Hand-peeled by skilled peelers" |
| Grade | "Alba grade, the finest Ceylon quills" |
| Flavour | "Sweet, floral and delicate, with no harsh bite" |
| Traceability | "Every pack traceable to its harvest lot" |
| Protected name | PGI messaging, only where entitled |

See [what is Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/what-is-ceylon-cinnamon) and [the flavour of Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-flavour-profile).

## Pick a target customer

- **Home bakers:** focus on flavour and baking performance. See [baking with Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/baking-with-ceylon-cinnamon).
- **Gourmet cooks:** origin, grade and culinary versatility.
- **Tea and coffee lovers:** infusion quality and sticks for drinks.
- **Gift buyers:** presentation, story and premium packaging. See [gift boxes and hampers](/blog/cinnamon-gift-boxes-hampers).
- **Conscious shoppers:** traceability and fair sourcing, with evidence. See [sustainable sourcing](/blog/sustainable-cinnamon-sourcing).

## Brand tiers

| Tier | Grade | Packaging | Message |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Everyday premium | C5, 60 mesh powder | Pouch, jar | "Real Ceylon cinnamon for every day" |
| Gourmet | C5 Special, 80 mesh | Glass jar, tube | "Single-origin, graded quills" |
| Luxury and gifting | Alba | Tin, gift box | "The finest grade, hand-rolled" |

## What to avoid

- **Health claims** such as references to blood sugar or weight. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).
- **Unverifiable superlatives** like "the world's best".
- **Misusing protected terms** or certification logos.
- **Inconsistent origin stories** across channels.

## Proof points that build trust

- Botanical name on pack.
- Grade and origin stated clearly.
- QR code or lot code linking to origin information.
- Real photos from origin. See [product photography](/blog/cinnamon-product-photography).
- Clear comparison with cassia, kept factual.
