**Cinnamon is classified under Harmonized System heading 0906, with 0906.11 for whole Ceylon cinnamon, 0906.19 for other whole cinnamon such as cassia, and 0906.20 for crushed or ground cinnamon.** The six-digit HS code is international; each country adds digits for its own tariff. Correct classification affects duty, statistics and sometimes inspection rates.

## The structure of heading 0906

| Code | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 0906 | Cinnamon and cinnamon-tree flowers |
| 0906.11 | Neither crushed nor ground: cinnamon (*Cinnamomum zeylanicum* Blume) |
| 0906.19 | Neither crushed nor ground: other |
| 0906.20 | Crushed or ground |

*Cinnamomum zeylanicum* Blume is a botanical synonym of *C. verum*, true Ceylon cinnamon. See [botanical names explained](/blog/cinnamomum-verum-vs-zeylanicum-names).

## Which code for which product

| Product | Likely HS subheading |
| --- | --- |
| Ceylon quills, whole or cut | 0906.11 |
| Ceylon quillings, featherings and chips (not ground) | Typically 0906.11 |
| Cassia sticks | 0906.19 |
| Ceylon cinnamon powder | 0906.20 |
| Cassia powder | 0906.20 |
| Spice blends with cinnamon | Often 0910, depending on composition |
| Cinnamon oils | Heading 3301 (essential oils) |

Classification of by-grades and blends can depend on specific facts and national practice. When in doubt, ask your broker or request a binding tariff ruling.

## National extensions

- **EU:** 8-digit Combined Nomenclature plus TARIC codes. See [importing into the EU](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-eu).
- **UK:** UK commodity codes under the UK Global Tariff. See [importing into the UK](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-uk).
- **US:** Harmonized Tariff Schedule of the United States (HTSUS), 10 digits. See [importing into the USA](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-usa-fda).

## Why it matters

- **Duty rates** may differ between codes or origins.
- **Preferential origin** claims must cite the correct code. See [REX and preferential tariffs](/blog/rex-certificate-origin-gsp).
- **Consistency:** the same code must appear on the invoice, packing list and certificates. See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).
- **Risk targeting:** customs and food authorities may target inspections by code and origin.
- **Trade statistics:** correct codes keep market data accurate.

## Summary

Use 0906.11 for whole Ceylon cinnamon, 0906.19 for whole cassia and other cinnamon, and 0906.20 for anything ground. Confirm national digits and rates before your first import, and keep the code identical across documents.
