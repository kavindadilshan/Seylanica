**Sri Lanka has a long history as a spice island, and today its spice export industry is led by Ceylon cinnamon, its most distinctive and globally recognised product.** For buyers, understanding the wider sector, its products, institutions and strengths, helps in sourcing confidently.

## Key spice exports

| Spice | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Ceylon cinnamon | Flagship; Sri Lanka is the main world source of *C. verum* |
| Black pepper | Known for high piperine content |
| Cloves | Aromatic, used in blends and oils |
| Nutmeg and mace | Grown in mid-country |
| Cardamom | Smaller volumes |
| Essential oils and oleoresins | Cinnamon bark and leaf oils, and others |

## Supporting institutions

- **Sri Lanka Export Development Board (EDB):** export promotion, branding such as the Pure Ceylon Cinnamon mark.
- **Department of Export Agriculture (DEA):** research, extension and training for export crops, including cinnamon.
- **Sri Lanka Standards Institution (SLSI):** national standards, including SLS 81 for cinnamon. See [SLS 81 explained](/blog/sls-81-standard-explained).
- **National Plant Quarantine Service (NPQS):** phytosanitary certification. See [the phytosanitary certificate](/blog/phytosanitary-certificate-npqs).
- **Industry associations** representing growers, processors and exporters.

## Strengths

- **Origin reputation:** "Ceylon" is a globally recognised quality name, protected as a PGI in the EU for cinnamon. See [PGI status](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-pgi-geographical-indication).
- **Skills:** generations of expertise in peeling and processing. See [the peeler's craft](/blog/cinnamon-peeling-craft).
- **Logistics:** the Port of Colombo's connectivity. See [shipping from Colombo](/blog/port-of-colombo-shipping).
- **Smallholder base** with diversified farming.
- **Trade preferences** in some markets. See [REX and preferential tariffs](/blog/rex-certificate-origin-gsp).

## Challenges

- **Labour:** the peeling skills gap. See [skills gap](/blog/cinnamon-skills-training-peelers).
- **Climate variability.** See [climate and supply](/blog/climate-impact-cinnamon-supply).
- **Compliance costs** for strict markets.
- **Value addition:** moving from bulk to branded and processed products.

## Opportunities for buyers

- **Direct sourcing** from traceable exporters. See [direct vs trader](/blog/buying-direct-from-origin-vs-trader).
- **Private label** and value-added products packed at origin. See [private label](/blog/private-label-ceylon-cinnamon-guide).
- **Long-term partnerships** for stable supply.
