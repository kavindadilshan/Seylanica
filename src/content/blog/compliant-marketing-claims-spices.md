**The safest and most effective way to market cinnamon is to sell what you can prove: species, origin, craft, grade, flavour and lab-tested quality, not health benefits.** Health claims on foods are strictly regulated in the EU, UK and US, and cinnamon "benefit" claims that circulate online are not authorised for food marketing.

## The rules in brief

| Market | Framework | What it means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EU | Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006 | Only authorised nutrition and health claims; general health references need an authorised claim alongside |
| UK (GB) | Retained nutrition and health claims rules | Only claims on the GB register |
| US | FDA rules; FTC advertising rules | Disease claims make a food an unapproved drug; claims must be truthful and substantiated |

This is a simplified overview; take regulatory advice for your specific claims and markets.

## Claims to avoid

- Anything about **blood sugar, diabetes, cholesterol, weight loss, inflammation, immunity** or **disease**.
- "Detox", "boosts metabolism", "superfood" with implied benefits.
- Testimonials implying health effects.
- Coumarin statements that imply health benefits. Factual statements about species are safer.

## What you can say (and should)

| Theme | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Species | "True cinnamon, *Cinnamomum verum*" |
| Origin | "Grown and hand-rolled in Sri Lanka" |
| Craft | "Hand-peeled by skilled peelers" |
| Grade | "Alba, the finest grade of Ceylon quills" |
| Flavour | "Sweet, floral and delicate" |
| Use | "Perfect in custards, chai and baking" |
| Quality | "Every lot lab-tested; lot code on pack" |
| Difference from cassia | "Many thin layers, not one thick bark" |

## Channels to review

- Labels and packaging.
- Website product pages and blogs.
- Marketplace listings. See [selling on Amazon](/blog/selling-cinnamon-on-amazon).
- Social media and paid ads.
- Influencer and affiliate content.
- Trade show materials.

## A simple review process

1. Keep a **claims register** listing every claim and its evidence.
2. **Pre-approve** all marketing copy against the register.
3. **Brief partners** and influencers in writing.
4. **Audit** live content periodically.
