**The best way to choose a Ceylon cinnamon supplier is to ask questions that require evidence: lot-specific documents, test reports, samples and references.** Anyone can describe their cinnamon as premium; a reliable supplier can prove it lot by lot. Here are ten questions to ask, and what good answers look like.

## 1. Where exactly does your cinnamon come from?

**Good answer:** a clear description of sourcing regions and growers, with lot numbers assigned at intake. See [garden-to-port traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).

**Warning sign:** vague answers or "we buy from the market".

## 2. Which grades do you supply, and to which standard?

**Good answer:** grades defined under SLS 81 with diameters and specs. See [grades explained](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-grades-guide).

## 3. Can I see a COA for the lot you are offering?

**Good answer:** yes, before order confirmation, with methods, limits and lab details. See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).

## 4. What do you test, and where?

**Good answer:** moisture, volatile oil, ash, microbiology for powder, pesticide residues, heavy metals and coumarin, with accredited labs for key tests.

## 5. Which documents come with each shipment?

**Good answer:** commercial invoice, packing list, B/L, certificate of origin, phytosanitary certificate, COA, and preferential origin statements where relevant. See [the export documents checklist](/blog/export-documents-ceylon-cinnamon).

## 6. Can you send samples from the actual lot?

**Good answer:** yes, labelled with the lot number. See [requesting samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples).

## 7. What are your MOQs, lead times and capacity?

**Good answer:** clear MOQs per product, realistic lead times, and honest capacity limits. See [MOQs explained](/blog/moq-ceylon-cinnamon).

## 8. What payment terms do you offer?

**Good answer:** standard trade terms such as T/T with deposit or L/C at sight, with bank details verified through a secure channel. See [payment terms](/blog/payment-terms-spice-trade).

## 9. How do you handle quality complaints?

**Good answer:** a defined process: joint testing, agreed tolerances, and remedies such as replacement or credit.

## 10. Who will I deal with, and how fast do you respond?

**Good answer:** a named contact, quick replies, and willingness to explain technical points. Responsiveness before the sale predicts responsiveness after it.

## Next steps

1. Shortlist two or three suppliers.
2. Request samples and COAs.
3. Test samples in your application.
4. Place a **trial order**.
