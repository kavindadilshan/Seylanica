**Sulphur dioxide (SO2) residues in cinnamon come from the traditional practice of exposing quills to burning sulphur to brighten their colour and deter pests.** Because sulphites are a regulated allergen in major markets, many importers now specify SO2 limits and require the value on every COA.

## Why sulphur fumigation was used

In traditional processing, quills were sometimes placed in closed chambers with burning sulphur. The fumes:

- **Lighten and even out colour**, which buyers associated with quality.
- **Deter insects and mould** in storage.

The practice is less common than in the past, and many processors avoid it, but buyers should check rather than assume.

## Why it matters to buyers

### Allergen labelling

Sulphites are on the list of allergens that must be declared:

- **EU:** sulphur dioxide and sulphites at concentrations of more than 10 mg/kg or 10 mg/litre, expressed as SO2, must be declared on the label.
- **US:** sulphites at 10 ppm or more in the finished food must be declared.

If cinnamon carries significant SO2 into your product, you may need allergen labelling, which most brands want to avoid.

### Additive rules

Whether sulphites are permitted as additives in a given food depends on market rules. Residues from processing can create compliance questions. Take advice for your product category.

### Sensory impact

Excessive SO2 can leave a sharp, sulphurous note. See [the flavour of Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-flavour-profile).

## How SO2 is tested

Labs typically use the optimised Monier-Williams distillation method or equivalent validated methods. Results are reported in mg/kg as SO2.

## What to specify

> Sulphur dioxide: maximum [your limit] mg/kg, determined by [method], reported per lot on the Certificate of Analysis. No sulphur fumigation [if required].

Many buyers choose a limit below the 10 mg/kg labelling threshold, to leave room for dosage in the final product.

## Alternatives to fumigation

- **Careful shade drying** to the right moisture. See [drying and processing cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-drying-processing).
- **Clean, dry, pest-controlled storage.**
- **Good packaging** with barrier liners.
- **Accepting natural colour variation** within the grade.

## Summary

SO2 is a legacy of traditional processing that now has labelling consequences. Ask your supplier about fumigation, specify a limit, and require SO2 on the COA.
