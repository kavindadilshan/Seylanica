**Importing Ceylon cinnamon smoothly depends on a complete, consistent set of documents.** Most delays at customs come from missing or mismatched paperwork, not from the product. Use this checklist to make sure every shipment has what it needs.

## Core commercial documents

| Document | Issued by | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commercial invoice | Exporter | Value, terms, HS code, buyer and seller details |
| Packing list | Exporter | Packages, net and gross weights, lot numbers |
| Bill of lading (sea) or airway bill (air) | Carrier | Receipt of goods, contract of carriage, title (B/L) |
| Insurance certificate | Seller under CIF, or buyer | Proof of cargo insurance |

## Origin and plant health

| Document | Issued by | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Certificate of origin | Authorised body in Sri Lanka | Proves Sri Lankan origin |
| Preferential origin statement | Registered exporter | Claims reduced tariffs where available (for example REX for the EU) |
| Phytosanitary certificate | National Plant Quarantine Service (NPQS) | Confirms plant health inspection |

See [the phytosanitary certificate](/blog/phytosanitary-certificate-npqs) and [certificates of origin and REX](/blog/rex-certificate-origin-gsp).

## Quality documents

| Document | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Certificate of Analysis (COA) | Lot test results: moisture, volatile oil, ash, microbiology, residues, metals, coumarin |
| Specification sheet | Agreed product specification |
| Safety Data Sheet (SDS) | Sometimes requested for powder or oils |
| Treatment certificate | If steam-sterilised or fumigated |

See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).

## Destination-side requirements

These are the importer's responsibility, but the exporter's data feeds them:

- **EU:** EORI number, customs declaration, food business registration, traceability records. See [importing into the EU](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-eu).
- **US:** FDA facility registration of the producer, Prior Notice, FSVP, ISF filing for ocean freight. See [importing into the USA](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-usa-fda).
- **UK:** EORI, customs declaration, plant health pre-notification where required. See [importing into the UK](/blog/importing-cinnamon-into-uk).

## Consistency checks before shipment

- Product description and **HS code** identical on invoice, packing list and certificates. See [HS codes for cinnamon](/blog/hs-codes-cinnamon).
- **Lot numbers** match the COA, packing list and bag labels.
- **Weights** agree across documents.
- **Consignee and notify party** details are correct.
- **Incoterm and place** are stated on the invoice.

## Letters of credit

Under an L/C, banks check documents strictly against the credit terms. Any discrepancy can delay payment. Share the draft L/C with your supplier before issuing it. See [payment terms in the spice trade](/blog/payment-terms-spice-trade).

## Summary

Good paperwork is invisible; bad paperwork costs days and money. Agree the document list at order stage, check consistency before shipment, and keep copies for traceability.
