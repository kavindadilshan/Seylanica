**A standard 20 ft container typically holds about 6 to 8 metric tonnes of Ceylon cinnamon quills, depending on grade and packing.** Because quills are light and bulky, containers usually fill up by volume before they hit weight limits. Powder and chips behave differently. Here is how to plan a load.

## Typical capacities

| Product | Packing | What limits the load | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quills (fine grades) | Lined cartons | Volume | Fragile; avoid crushing |
| Quills (thicker grades) | Cartons or pressed bales | Volume | Bales increase tonnage |
| Chips and quillings | 25 kg PP bags with liners | Volume or weight | Can be bulk-stacked |
| Powder | 25 kg multi-wall paper bags with liners | Weight or pallet layout | Denser product |
| Retail packs | Cartons on pallets | Pallet count | Plan by case count |

Our planning figure for quills is about 6 to 8 MT in a 20 ft container. A 40 ft container holds roughly twice the volume, though the tonnage depends on packing. Ask for a **load plan** with your quote; the [shipping planner](/buyer-hub#shipping) in our Buyer Hub gives an instant estimate.

## FCL vs LCL

- **FCL (full container load):** lowest cost per kilo, sealed at origin, less handling.
- **LCL (less than container load):** for smaller orders; shared space, more handling, higher cost per kilo, more risk of damage and contamination.

For orders below a few tonnes, LCL or air may make sense. See [sea vs air freight](/blog/shipping-cinnamon-sea-vs-air).

## Protecting cinnamon in the container

### Moisture and condensation

Sea containers heat up by day and cool at night. Moisture in the air condenses on the roof and walls and can drip onto cargo ("container rain"). To prevent damage:

- Load only product at specification moisture. See [moisture in cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).
- Use **container desiccants**.
- Line floor and walls with **kraft paper** or use liners.
- Ensure the container is **clean, dry and odour-free** before loading.

### Crushing

Fine quills such as Alba break under weight. Use rigid cartons, limit stacking height and place heavier goods at the bottom.

### Odour taint

Cinnamon absorbs odours. Never load with chemicals, fuels or strong-smelling cargo, and inspect the container for previous cargo odours.

### Security

Use high-security seals and record seal numbers on the bill of lading.

## Packing options

- **Vacuum-packed inner bags** inside cartons for fine quills.
- **Pressed bales** for thicker grades, saving space.
- **Palletised** loads for retail packs and powder, for faster unloading.
- **Floor-loaded** bags for chips, maximising space.
