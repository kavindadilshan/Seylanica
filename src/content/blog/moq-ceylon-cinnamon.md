**Minimum order quantities (MOQs) exist because every export order carries fixed costs: grading and sorting, lot testing, documentation, inspection and shipping.** Below a certain volume, those costs make the price per kilo uneconomic. Understanding MOQs helps you plan orders and negotiate sensibly.

## Why MOQs exist

| Fixed cost per order or lot | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Grading and sorting | Selecting quills to grade |
| Testing | COA panel per lot |
| Documents | Certificates of origin, phytosanitary inspection |
| Packing | Setting up packing lines and labels |
| Logistics | Container booking, customs, trucking |

## Typical MOQs

Seylanica's indicative MOQs:

| Product | MOQ |
| --- | --- |
| Alba quills | 100 kg |
| C5 Special quills | 200 kg |
| C5 and C4 quills | 500 kg |
| Powder | 250 kg |
| Chips and broken quills | 1 MT |

Finer grades have lower MOQs because buyers typically need smaller quantities and the value per kilo is higher. See our [product catalog](/products) for each product's terms.

## Options for smaller buyers

- **Combine products** in one order to justify shipping costs.
- **Start with retail or trade packs** (50 g to 1 kg) via our [shop](/shop).
- **Use LCL sea freight or air** for smaller volumes. See [sea vs air freight](/blog/shipping-cinnamon-sea-vs-air).
- **Order samples first**, then a trial order. See [your first import order](/blog/first-cinnamon-import-order-guide).
- **Partner** with another buyer to share a container.

## MOQs and price tiers

Prices usually step down at volume thresholds, such as from hundreds of kilos to a tonne, and again at container loads. Ask for **volume tier pricing** so you can see where the savings are. See [what determines the price of Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-price-factors).

## Negotiating MOQs

- Explain your **growth plan**; suppliers may accept smaller first orders for a credible long-term buyer.
- Offer **flexibility** on packing or shipping dates.
- Consider an **annual agreement** with scheduled call-offs. See [annual contracts vs spot buying](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).

## Summary

MOQs are about covering fixed costs, not about turning small buyers away. Combine products, start with samples and trial orders, and plan towards volume tiers. [Request a quote](/quote) with your expected annual volume and we will suggest the most efficient way to start.
