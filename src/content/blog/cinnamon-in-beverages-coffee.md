**Ceylon cinnamon is a natural partner for coffee, milk and chocolate, adding sweetness and floral warmth without cassia's harsh edge.** The key to great cinnamon drinks is choosing the right format: sticks for infusion, fine powder for dusting, and syrups for consistent flavour at speed.

## Formats and their best uses

| Format | Best for | Tip |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cut sticks (5 to 8 cm) | Garnish, stirrers, infusion | Serve with hot drinks |
| Whole quills | Simmered drinks, syrups, batch infusion | Break into pieces for faster extraction |
| Fine powder (80 mesh) | Dusting, mixes, shakers | Mix with sugar to avoid clumps |
| Syrup | Lattes, cold drinks | Make from quills for clean flavour |

See [cut stick formats](/blog/cut-cinnamon-sticks-sizes) and [powder mesh sizes](/blog/cinnamon-powder-mesh-size).

## Drink ideas

- **Cinnamon latte:** espresso, steamed milk, Ceylon cinnamon syrup, dusting of powder.
- **Café de olla style coffee:** coffee simmered with a cinnamon stick and unrefined sugar.
- **Masala chai:** black tea simmered with milk, cinnamon, cardamom, ginger and clove. See [tea blending](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-tea-blending).
- **Hot chocolate:** infuse milk with a stick before adding chocolate.
- **Cold brew with cinnamon:** steep a broken quill with the coffee grounds.
- **Horchata-style drinks:** rice or almond base with cinnamon.

## Simple Ceylon cinnamon syrup

1. Combine water and sugar in equal weights.
2. Add broken Ceylon quills (C4 or C5 work well).
3. Simmer gently, then steep off the heat.
4. Strain, cool and refrigerate.

Adjust strength by quill quantity and steeping time. See [infusions, syrups and spirits](/blog/infusing-cinnamon-syrups-spirits).

## Dispersion tips

- Cinnamon powder **does not dissolve**; mix with sugar or a small amount of liquid first.
- Use **80 mesh** for minimal grit.
- For **cold drinks**, use syrup or infusion rather than powder.
