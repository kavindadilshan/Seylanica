## Packaging tips

- **Protect sticks** from breakage with tubes or inserts.
- **Seal for aroma** with barrier liners, even in decorative tins.
- **Include a story card** about origin, the peeler's craft and how to use the cinnamon. See [the peeler's craft](/blog/cinnamon-peeling-craft).
- **Use recyclable materials** where possible.

See [cinnamon packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).

## Compliance in gift packs

- Each food item still needs **mandatory labelling** appropriate to the market, either on the item or on the outer pack, depending on how it is sold. See [labelling requirements](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
- **No health claims** in story cards or packaging. See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).
- Check **best-before dates** across mixed items.
- Consider **allergen cross-contact** if mixing with nuts, chocolate or other foods.

## Seasonal planning

1. **Decide the range** early in the year.
2. **Order packaging** with long enough lead time for printing.
3. **Order cinnamon** allowing for production and transit. See [planning around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).
4. **Assemble and test** transit performance.
5. **Launch** with enough time for retail buyers and online promotion.

## Corporate gifting

Businesses value gifts that are useful, premium and tell a story. Offer:

- Custom-branded tins or sleeves.
- Origin story cards.
- Tiered options by budget.
