**A sustainable retail price for Ceylon cinnamon starts with your full landed and packed cost, adds channel margins, and is then checked against competing Ceylon products.** Because Ceylon costs more than cassia, your price must be explained by value, not hidden by discounting.

## Step 1: Landed cost of the cinnamon

Per kilo, include:

- Product price at your Incoterm.
- Freight, insurance and origin charges.
- Duties and customs clearance.
- Inland transport.
- Testing on arrival.
- Losses (breakage, sampling).

See [what determines the price of Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-price-factors).

## Step 2: Packed cost per unit

| Cost line | Example items |
| --- | --- |
| Cinnamon content | Net weight × landed cost per kg |
| Primary pack | Jar, tube, pouch, tin |
| Closure and label | Lid, shaker, printed label |
| Secondary pack | Case, shrink |
| Packing labour | If packing locally |
| Allowance | Waste and rework |

In small premium packs, packaging can exceed the cost of the cinnamon. See [packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options).

## Step 3: Overheads and your margin

Marketing, warehousing, admin, certification, returns and your profit.

## Step 4: Channel margins

| Channel | Considerations |
| --- | --- |
| Supermarket | Retailer margin, promotions, listing fees |
| Speciality and delicatessen | Higher price points, smaller volumes |
| Distributor | Distributor margin plus retailer margin |
| Marketplace | Fees, fulfilment, advertising. See [selling on Amazon](/blog/selling-cinnamon-on-amazon) |
| Direct to consumer | Shipping, payment fees, acquisition costs. See [D2C spice brands](/blog/subscription-spice-business) |

## Step 5: Sense-check against the market

- Compare price **per 100 g** with other Ceylon products in the same tier.
- Check that your positioning justifies your price. See [brand positioning](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-brand-positioning).

## Pack size strategy

- **Small packs** (50 g sticks, 100 g powder): high price per kilo, impulse and gifting.
- **Medium packs** (250 g): regular users, better value.
- **Large packs** (500 g to 1 kg): bakers, food service, value seekers.

## Good-better-best by grade

| Tier | Product |
| --- | --- |
| Good | C5 cut sticks or 60 mesh powder |
| Better | C5 Special sticks or 80 mesh powder |
| Best | Alba sticks in a tin or tube |

See [C5 Special vs C5](/blog/c5-special-vs-c5-cinnamon) and [Alba cinnamon](/blog/alba-cinnamon-grade).
