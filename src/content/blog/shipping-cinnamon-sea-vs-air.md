**Ship by sea for commercial volumes and by air for samples and urgent small orders.** Sea freight costs a fraction of air freight per kilo, but it takes weeks rather than days. The right choice depends on volume, urgency and the value of the product.

## Comparing the modes

| | Sea freight (FCL/LCL) | Air freight | Courier (express) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Typical use | Commercial orders | Urgent orders, launches | Samples, documents |
| Cost per kg | Lowest | High | Highest |
| Transit | Weeks | Days | Days |
| Volume | Tonnes | Kilos to a few tonnes | Small parcels |
| Incoterms | FOB, CFR, CIF | FCA, CPT, CIP, DAP | DAP |
| Handling risk | Condensation, long transit | Less time in transit | Less time in transit |

## Sea freight from Colombo

Colombo is a major transhipment hub with frequent services to Europe, the Middle East, North America and Asia. See [shipping from the Port of Colombo](/blog/port-of-colombo-shipping).

**Best for:** container loads and LCL consignments of quills, chips and powder.

**Plan for:**

- Port-to-port transit of several weeks, depending on route and transhipment.
- Time for documents, inspection and customs at both ends.
- Moisture protection in the container. See [container loading](/blog/container-loading-cinnamon).

## Air freight

**Best for:** urgent replenishment, product launches, high-value retail stock.

**Plan for:** higher cost, volumetric weight charges (light, bulky quills can be charged by volume), and airport handling.

## Courier for samples

Samples of 100 to 250 g per grade are typically sent by express courier on DAP terms. Include a commercial invoice, and check whether your country requires any documents for food samples. See [requesting cinnamon samples](/blog/requesting-cinnamon-samples).

## Choosing the right mode

1. **Volume under about 100 kg:** courier or air.
2. **Volume of a few hundred kg to a few tonnes:** LCL sea, or air if urgent.
3. **Volume of several tonnes or more:** FCL sea.
4. **Launch or stock-out:** split shipment with a small air consignment and the balance by sea.

## Landed cost thinking

Compare **total landed cost**, not just freight:

- Freight and surcharges.
- Insurance.
- Origin and destination handling.
- Customs clearance and duties.
- Inland delivery.
- Inventory carrying cost of slower transit.

Sea for scale, air for speed, courier for samples. Build sea transit into your buying calendar so you rarely need expensive air shipments. See [planning around lead times and harvests](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon), and [request a quote](/quote) with your preferred mode.
