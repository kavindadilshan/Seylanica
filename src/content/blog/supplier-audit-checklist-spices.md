**A supplier audit tells you whether good product is the result of reliable systems or luck.** For Ceylon cinnamon, the key areas are traceability, hygienic drying and processing, moisture and pest control, testing and release, and food fraud prevention. Use this checklist as a starting point for your own programme.

## 1. Company and scope

- [ ] Legal entity, export registration and contact details.
- [ ] Products and grades in scope.
- [ ] Sites: collection, processing, milling, packing, storage.
- [ ] Certifications held, with certificate numbers and validity, verified with the issuer.

## 2. Traceability

- [ ] Lot numbers assigned at intake.
- [ ] Records linking growers or collectors to lots.
- [ ] Lot numbers on packaging, invoices, COAs.
- [ ] Mock recall performed and timed.

See [garden-to-port traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).

## 3. Raw material control

- [ ] Grower agreements or approved collector lists.
- [ ] Guidance on Good Agricultural Practices and input use.
- [ ] Intake inspection: moisture, mould, foreign matter.

## 4. Processing hygiene

- [ ] Clean peeling and grading areas, protected from animals and weather.
- [ ] Drying on raised racks, under cover. See [drying and processing](/blog/cinnamon-drying-processing).
- [ ] Hand-washing facilities and worker hygiene rules.
- [ ] Food-grade contact surfaces and packaging.
- [ ] Glass and hard plastics policy.

## 5. Moisture, storage and pests

- [ ] Moisture testing before packing. See [moisture in cinnamon](/blog/cinnamon-moisture-content-iso-939).
- [ ] Dry, ventilated storage off the floor.
- [ ] Pest control programme with records.
- [ ] Segregation of products and allergens, if any.

## 6. Milling (if powder)

- [ ] Equipment cleaning schedules.
- [ ] Metal detection or magnets.
- [ ] Temperature control during milling.
- [ ] Treatment validation if steam-sterilised. See [steam sterilisation](/blog/steam-sterilisation-vs-irradiation-spices).

## 7. Testing and release

- [ ] Testing plan per product.
- [ ] Accredited labs for microbiology, residues and metals.
- [ ] Positive release: no lot shipped before results are in spec.
- [ ] Retained samples kept per lot.

See [what a COA should include](/blog/certificate-of-analysis-cinnamon).

## 8. Food fraud and authenticity

- [ ] Vulnerability assessment for substitution with cassia.
- [ ] Coumarin testing to confirm species.
- [ ] Controls on bought-in material.

See [cinnamon adulteration](/blog/cinnamon-adulteration-detection).

## 9. Documents and complaints

- [ ] Export document process and checks.
- [ ] Complaint log with root-cause analysis.
- [ ] Corrective and preventive action records.

## Scoring and follow-up

Rate each section (for example: compliant, minor, major, critical). Agree corrective actions with deadlines, and set a re-audit frequency based on risk and performance.
