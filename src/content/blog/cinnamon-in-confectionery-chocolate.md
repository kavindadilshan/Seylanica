**Ceylon cinnamon's floral sweetness makes it a natural partner for chocolate, caramel, dairy and fruit, adding warmth without the hot edge of cassia.** For confectionery and dessert makers, the keys are fine particle size, the right infusion technique, and attention to coumarin and microbiology.

## Great pairings

| Pairing | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Chocolate | Mexican-style hot chocolate, cinnamon truffles, spiced bars |
| Caramel | Cinnamon caramel sauces, toffees |
| Nuts | Cinnamon almonds, pralines, nut brittles |
| Fruit | Apple, pear, plum, citrus desserts |
| Dairy | Custards, panna cotta, rice pudding, ice cream |
| Coffee | Mocha desserts, tiramisu variations |

## Formats and techniques

- **Infusion:** steep quills in cream or milk for ganache, custards and ice cream bases, then strain. Clean flavour, no grit.
- **Fine powder (80 mesh or finer):** for chocolate, coatings and dustings. See [powder mesh](/blog/cinnamon-powder-mesh-size).
- **Cinnamon sugar:** for coating nuts, churros and doughnuts.
- **Syrups:** for drizzles and soaking. See [infusions and syrups](/blog/infusing-cinnamon-syrups-spirits).

## Processing tips

- **Chocolate:** add powder during refining or use infused cocoa butter for smoothness; test viscosity effects.
- **Ice cream:** infuse during base pasteurisation for best results.
- **Hard candy and toffee:** add late to limit aroma loss from high heat.
- **Coatings:** pre-mix with sugar for even distribution.

## Compliance notes

### Coumarin

In the EU, the maximum coumarin level in **desserts** is 5 mg/kg, one of the lowest food limits under Regulation (EC) No 1334/2008. Ceylon's naturally low coumarin is a practical advantage. See [coumarin explained](/blog/coumarin-in-cinnamon-explained).

### Microbiology

Many confections and desserts have no heat step after cinnamon is added. Use **steam-sterilised** powder or validate your process. See [steam sterilisation](/blog/steam-sterilisation-vs-irradiation-spices).

### Allergens

Cinnamon itself is not a major listed allergen, but chocolate and dessert facilities often handle milk, nuts and soy. Manage cross-contact and labelling. See [labelling](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
