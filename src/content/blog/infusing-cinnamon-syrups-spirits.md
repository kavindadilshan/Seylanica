**Ceylon quills make clean, aromatic infusions for syrups, cordials and spirits, with a floral sweetness and less bitterness than cassia.** The secrets are breaking the quills for even extraction, controlling time and temperature, and straining well.

## Basic principles

- **Use quills, not powder,** for clear liquids.
- **Break quills** into pieces to increase surface area.
- **Control temperature:** hot infusions extract quickly; cold infusions are gentler.
- **Taste often:** over-extraction turns woody and tannic.
- **Strain thoroughly** through fine mesh or filter paper.

## Cinnamon syrup

1. Combine equal weights of water and sugar.
2. Add broken Ceylon quills.
3. Bring to a gentle simmer, then remove from heat.
4. Steep until the flavour is right.
5. Strain, cool and refrigerate.

Variations: add vanilla, orange peel or cardamom. See [beverages](/blog/cinnamon-in-beverages-coffee).

## Cold infusions

Steep broken quills in cold water, milk or cold brew coffee for several hours in the fridge for a soft, rounded flavour.

## Spirit infusions

Ceylon cinnamon pairs well with rum, whisky, brandy and aged spirits, and with gin botanicals.

- Add broken quills to the spirit in a clean, sealed jar.
- Taste regularly and remove the cinnamon when the flavour is right.
- Filter before bottling.

Commercial producers must follow the applicable spirit drink and labelling rules in their market.

## Scaling up for producers

| Variable | Fix it in your SOP |
| --- | --- |
| Raw material | Grade, cut size, volatile oil per lot |
| Ratio | Grams of cinnamon per litre |
| Temperature | Exact range |
| Time | Exact duration |
| Agitation | Method and frequency |
| Filtration | Filter grade |

Specify volatile oil to keep strength consistent across lots. See [volatile oil explained](/blog/volatile-oil-cinnamon-iso-6571).

## Which grade?

- **C4 or C5** quills give good flavour at sensible cost for infusion. See [C4 cinnamon](/blog/c4-cinnamon-grade-uses).
- **Chips** offer the lowest cost per extraction for industrial use. See [chips for extraction](/blog/cinnamon-chips-industrial-extraction).
- **Alba or C5 Special** sticks for garnish.

## Quality and safety

- Use **clean, food-grade equipment**.
- For non-alcoholic syrups, follow good hygiene and refrigeration; validate shelf life.
- Check **coumarin** if your product category has limits. See [coumarin explained](/blog/coumarin-in-cinnamon-explained).
