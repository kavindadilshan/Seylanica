**Private label means selling Ceylon cinnamon under your own brand while a specialist supplier sources, packs and often labels it for you.** It is the fastest way to build a cinnamon brand without owning processing facilities. Here is how to plan a launch.

## Step 1: Define the product

| Decision | Options |
| --- | --- |
| Format | Cut sticks, whole quills, powder, gift sets |
| Grade | Alba, C5 Special, C5. See [grades explained](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-grades-guide) |
| Pack size | 50 g, 100 g, 250 g, 500 g, 1 kg |
| Packaging | Tubes, jars, pouches, tins. See [packaging options](/blog/cinnamon-packaging-options) |
| Positioning | Everyday, premium, gourmet, gifting. See [brand positioning](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-brand-positioning) |
| Price point | Work backwards from retail. See [retail pricing](/blog/retail-pricing-cinnamon) |

## Step 2: Choose where to pack

- **At origin:** lower cost, origin story, fewer handling steps. Needs artwork and compliance approved upfront.
- **Locally:** flexibility, faster artwork changes, smaller runs. Bulk import plus local packing costs.

## Step 3: Packaging and artwork

- Choose **food-contact compliant** materials with good aroma barrier.
- Design artwork with **mandatory label elements** first, then branding. See [labelling for the EU, UK and US](/blog/cinnamon-label-requirements-eu-us).
- Agree **who supplies packaging**: the packer, a local printer, or you.
- Allow time for **print proofs** and printer lead times.

## Step 4: Compliance

As brand owner, you are usually the business responsible for the label in your market. Make sure:

- Claims are accurate and **free of health claims**. See [marketing spices without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices).
- Origin and PGI references are **supported by documents**. See [PGI status](/blog/ceylon-cinnamon-pgi-geographical-indication).
- **Lot coding** links each pack to an export lot and COA.
- **Best-before** dates are justified. See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

## Step 5: Samples and pilot run

- Approve **product samples** and a **packed prototype**.
- Run a **pilot batch** to check fill weights, seals and transit performance.
- Test with real customers if possible.

## Step 6: Launch and scale

- Start with **two or three SKUs**.
- Track sell-through, reviews and repeat purchases.
- Add formats such as gift sets or larger packs once core SKUs perform.

## What a good private-label partner offers

- Consistent grades and lot COAs.
- Packing options from tubes to trade packs.
- Help with label content and documentation.
- Realistic MOQs and lead times.
- Clear communication.

## Summary

Private label is a practical route into the premium cinnamon category: define the product, get packaging and compliance right, pilot, then scale.
