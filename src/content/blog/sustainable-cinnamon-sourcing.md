**Sustainable cinnamon sourcing means buying in a way that protects the land, supports the people who grow and peel the bark, and can be proven through traceable records.** For buyers, it is increasingly a customer expectation and, in some markets, a regulatory one.

## Three pillars

| Pillar | Key questions |
| --- | --- |
| Environment | How are soils, water and biodiversity managed? How are inputs used? |
| People | Do growers earn fair returns? Are peelers paid fairly and working safely? |
| Transparency | Can lots be traced to growing areas? Are claims backed by evidence? |

## Environment

- **Good Agricultural Practices** and responsible input use. See [cultivation and coppicing](/blog/cinnamon-cultivation-coppicing).
- **Soil conservation** on slopes and organic matter management.
- **Biodiversity:** mixed cropping and shade trees in some gardens.
- **Waste reduction:** using chips, featherings and leaves. See [by-products](/blog/cinnamon-by-products-zero-waste).
- **Climate resilience.** See [weather, climate and supply](/blog/climate-impact-cinnamon-supply).

## People

- **Fair returns to smallholders.** See [smallholders in the supply chain](/blog/smallholders-cinnamon-supply-chain).
- **Fair pay and safe conditions for peelers**, including safe tools and reasonable hours. See [the skills gap](/blog/cinnamon-skills-training-peelers).
- **No child or forced labour** anywhere in the chain.
- **Training and career paths** to keep the craft alive.

## Transparency

- **Lot-level traceability.** See [garden-to-port traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).
- **Supplier codes of conduct** and self-assessments.
- **Audits** where appropriate. See [supplier audit checklist](/blog/supplier-audit-checklist-spices).

## Certifications

Organic, Fairtrade and Rainforest Alliance are among schemes that can apply to spices. Use them where they fit your market, and only claim them when held. See [organic Ceylon cinnamon](/blog/organic-ceylon-cinnamon).

## Making credible claims

Regulators in several markets are tightening rules on environmental claims. Avoid vague terms like "eco-friendly" or "sustainable" without specific, evidenced support. Better:

- "Traceable to its harvest lot in southern Sri Lanka."
- "Packed in recyclable [material] pouches."

See [marketing without health claims](/blog/compliant-marketing-claims-spices) for the same principle applied to health.

## What buyers can do

1. Choose **traceable suppliers**.
2. Build **long-term relationships** with stable volumes. See [annual contracts](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).
3. **Pay fair prices** that reflect skilled labour.
4. **Ask questions** and share expectations in writing.
5. **Report honestly** on what you know and what you are still improving.
