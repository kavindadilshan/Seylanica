**Ceylon cinnamon is only as plentiful as the skilled peelers who make it, and that workforce is shrinking and ageing.** The skills gap is one of the most important long-term factors behind Ceylon cinnamon's supply and price.

## Why the gap exists

- **Demanding work:** peeling is physically demanding and requires years to master.
- **Income perception:** younger people may see better-paid or more prestigious options elsewhere.
- **Seasonality:** work follows the harvest, making income uneven.
- **Status:** the craft has not always been valued as skilled work.

## Effects on the industry

| Effect | Why |
| --- | --- |
| Rising labour costs | Fewer peelers, more demand for their time |
| Supply constraints | Bark cannot become quills without peelers |
| Grade mix shifts | Fine grades like Alba need the most skilled hands |
| Quality variation | Less experienced peelers produce more breakage and uneven quills |

See [why Ceylon cinnamon costs more](/blog/why-ceylon-cinnamon-costs-more) and [Alba cinnamon](/blog/alba-cinnamon-grade).

## Responses

### Training

Government agencies, including Sri Lanka's Department of Export Agriculture, and industry bodies have supported training for peelers, alongside traditional learning in families and communities.

### Better conditions

Fair pay, safer tools, better workspaces and recognition of peeling as skilled work help attract and keep peelers.

### Mechanical aids

Tools and machines that assist with scraping and some peeling steps are being developed and used. Rolling the finest quills remains largely manual.

### Productivity

Better organisation of harvest and processing helps peelers work more efficiently.

## What buyers can do

- **Pay for quality**, recognising the skill in fine grades.
- **Buy consistently**, supporting stable work.
- **Support traceable suppliers** who can link value to the people doing the work. See [sustainable sourcing](/blog/sustainable-cinnamon-sourcing).
- **Tell the story**, with consent and accuracy. See [product photography](/blog/cinnamon-product-photography).
