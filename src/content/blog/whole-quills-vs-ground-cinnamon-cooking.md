**Use whole cinnamon sticks when you want slow, gentle infusion or a visual garnish; use ground cinnamon when you want immediate, even flavour throughout a dish.** Ceylon cinnamon's brittle quills make it easy to switch between the two, and grinding fresh gives the best of both.

## Side-by-side

| | Whole quills (sticks) | Ground cinnamon |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flavour release | Slow, controllable | Immediate |
| Best for | Infusions, simmering, poaching, drinks | Baking, dusting, rubs, blends |
| Texture | Removed before eating | Disperses into food |
| Shelf life | Longer aroma retention | Fades faster |
| Presentation | Attractive garnish | Invisible |

## When to use sticks

- **Infusing liquids:** milk for custards, syrups, poaching liquids, mulled drinks.
- **Simmered dishes:** curries, stews, rice. See [Sri Lankan cooking](/blog/sri-lankan-dishes-cinnamon).
- **Drinks:** stirring sticks for coffee, chai and hot chocolate. See [beverages](/blog/cinnamon-in-beverages-coffee).
- **Presentation:** gift packs and garnishes.

## When to use ground

- **Baking:** batters, doughs and fillings. See [baking with Ceylon](/blog/baking-with-ceylon-cinnamon).
- **Dusting:** on lattes, desserts and toast.
- **Rubs and blends.** See [spice blends](/blog/cinnamon-spice-blends).
- **Quick dishes** where there is no time to infuse.

## Converting between them

There is no exact conversion, because infusion time and dish matter. As a starting point, use one 5 to 8 cm stick in place of a small amount of ground cinnamon in a simmered dish, then adjust to taste. For baking, use ground; sticks will not distribute.

## Grind fresh for best flavour

Ceylon quills crumble easily, so a spice grinder turns them into powder in seconds. Grinding small amounts as needed gives the brightest aroma. Sift if you want a fine texture.

## Storage

- **Sticks:** airtight, cool, dark. They keep aroma well.
- **Powder:** airtight, cool, dark, and use within its best-before.

See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

## For professional kitchens and manufacturers

- Buy **quills** for infusion-heavy menus and in-house grinding.
- Buy **powder** with a specified mesh for baking and dry mixes. See [powder mesh](/blog/cinnamon-powder-mesh-size).
- Consider **cost per use**: sticks can sometimes be reused in batch infusions.
