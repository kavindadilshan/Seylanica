**Running out of cinnamon is usually a planning problem, not a supply problem.** Total lead time from order to usable stock includes production and testing at origin, sea transit, customs and your own QA release. Add seasonal demand peaks and harvest timing, and a simple buying calendar becomes essential.

## The full lead-time chain

| Stage | Typical duration |
| --- | --- |
| Sample evaluation (new products) | 1 to 3 weeks |
| Production, grading, testing, documents | 2 to 5 weeks after PO and deposit |
| Booking and loading | Depends on sailings |
| Sea transit | Several weeks, by route |
| Customs and delivery | Days to a couple of weeks |
| Your QA testing and release | Days to two weeks |

Our product lead times: quills three to five weeks, chips two to four weeks, powder two to three weeks after PO and deposit. See our [catalog](/products).

## Harvest timing

Ceylon cinnamon has main harvest periods linked to the monsoon rains, when bark peels easily. Supply of fresh material is best after these periods; availability of specific grades can tighten between them. See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season).

## Demand peaks

- **Festive season** baking and mulled drinks in many markets.
- **Autumn** spiced beverages.
- **Religious festivals** in key markets.
- **Product launches** and promotions.

## Building a buying calendar

1. **Forecast monthly demand** per grade.
2. **Set safety stock** for each grade.
3. **Work backwards** from need dates using the full lead time.
4. **Align** larger orders with post-harvest availability.
5. **Use contracts** for base volume. See [annual contracts vs spot](/blog/annual-supply-contracts-cinnamon).
6. **Review monthly**.

## Safety stock

Safety stock protects against:

- Port congestion and transhipment delays.
- Weather disruptions at origin.
- Unexpected demand spikes.
- QA holds on arrival.

Cinnamon quills store well when kept cool, dry and sealed, so holding stock is practical. See [shelf life and storage](/blog/cinnamon-shelf-life-storage).

## When things go wrong

- **Split shipments:** a small air shipment to bridge a gap, the rest by sea. See [sea vs air](/blog/shipping-cinnamon-sea-vs-air).
- **Grade substitution:** temporarily use an adjacent grade, with customer approval.
- **Local top-ups** from a trader. See [direct vs trader](/blog/buying-direct-from-origin-vs-trader).

## Summary

Map the whole chain, respect the harvest calendar, and hold sensible safety stock. A simple calendar avoids expensive air freight and empty shelves. Share your annual forecast when you [request a quote](/quote), and we will propose a shipment schedule.
