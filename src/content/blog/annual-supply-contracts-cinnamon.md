**Spot buying gives you flexibility; annual contracts give you stability.** For a product with seasonal supply, a limited growing area and moving exchange rates, many Ceylon cinnamon buyers use a mix: a contracted base volume for predictability, plus spot purchases for flexibility.

## Comparing the approaches

| | Spot buying | Annual or seasonal contract |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | Market price at the time | Fixed or formula-based for agreed periods |
| Supply security | Lower | Higher; contracted volumes prioritised |
| Flexibility | High | Lower |
| Planning | Order by order | Scheduled call-offs |
| Admin | Per order | Upfront negotiation, simpler orders later |
| Relationship | Transactional | Partnership |

## When spot buying works

- Small or irregular volumes.
- Uncertain demand, for example during a product launch.
- When you have alternative suppliers and stock.

## When contracts work

- Regular, forecastable demand.
- Products where **cinnamon cost is a key margin driver**.
- Retail or manufacturing commitments with **fixed prices** downstream.
- When **consistent quality** across the year matters.

## Structuring a supply contract

1. **Products and specifications.** Reference your agreed spec. See [writing a purchase specification](/blog/writing-cinnamon-purchase-specification).
2. **Volume.** Annual volume, with minimum and maximum, and call-off schedule.
3. **Price mechanism.** Fixed per shipment window, or base price with review triggers (for example exchange rate moves beyond an agreed band).
4. **Incoterm and port.** See [Incoterms](/blog/incoterms-for-spice-importers).
5. **Payment terms.** See [payment terms](/blog/payment-terms-spice-trade).
6. **Quality and remedies.** Sampling, tolerances, referee lab, replacement or credit.
7. **Documents.** Per-lot COA and export documents.
8. **Force majeure.** Weather, harvest failure, port disruption.
9. **Term and renewal.**

## A hybrid strategy

- **Contract 60 to 80%** of forecast volume.
- **Buy the rest spot**, using market dips or for new products.
- **Review quarterly** against actual demand.

## Aligning with the harvest

Schedule larger shipments after main harvest periods, when fresh supply is available. See [harvest seasons](/blog/cinnamon-harvest-season) and [planning around lead times](/blog/lead-times-planning-cinnamon).
