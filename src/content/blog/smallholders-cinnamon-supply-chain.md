**Much of the world's Ceylon cinnamon starts on small family holdings in Sri Lanka's wet zone.** Between those gardens and an importer's warehouse stand peelers, collectors, processors and exporters, each adding value. Understanding this chain helps buyers source responsibly and reliably.

## The chain

| Link | Role | Value added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Smallholder grower | Cultivates and harvests shoots | Raw material |
| Peeler | Scrapes, peels and rolls quills | Craft, grade |
| Collector or dealer | Aggregates from many growers | Logistics, volume |
| Processor | Grades, dries, sorts, mills, packs | Quality and consistency |
| Exporter | Tests, documents, ships | Compliance, market access |
| Importer or brand | Distributes, packs, markets | Market reach |

Some businesses combine several of these roles.

## Challenges for smallholders

- **Price volatility** when demand or currency shifts.
- **Labour shortages** in peeling. See [the skills gap](/blog/cinnamon-skills-training-peelers).
- **Weather risk.** See [climate and supply](/blog/climate-impact-cinnamon-supply).
- **Limited market information** about grades and prices.
- **Compliance costs** of meeting export standards.

## Strengths of smallholder supply

- **Generations of knowledge** in cultivation and peeling.
- **Diversified land use** with mixed crops.
- **Authentic origin stories** for brands.

## How buyers can help

1. **Buy consistently** rather than only in spot spikes.
2. **Support traceability** so value can be linked back to growers. See [garden-to-port traceability](/blog/from-garden-to-port-traceability).
3. **Pay for quality**, rewarding well-peeled, well-dried lots.
4. **Share forecasts** so suppliers can plan purchases from growers.
5. **Engage with sustainability** in practical ways. See [sustainable sourcing](/blog/sustainable-cinnamon-sourcing).

## Telling the story honestly

Smallholder stories are powerful in marketing, but must be accurate. Use real information, real photos with consent, and avoid implying direct farm relationships you do not have. See [product photography](/blog/cinnamon-product-photography).

## Summary

Ceylon cinnamon is a smallholder crop elevated by skilled hands. Buyers who source consistently, value traceability and pay for quality help keep that system strong.
